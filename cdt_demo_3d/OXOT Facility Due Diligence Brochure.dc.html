OPERATIONS SAFE, BY DESIGN
Collection is passive. Nothing is scanned actively on a live process network, no device is probed in a way that risks its function, and every step is agreed with the control-system owner before it happens. ENGAGEMENT TIMELINE
SIX STEPS, PER FACILITY

How the assessment actually runs.

The same six steps at every site, so findings are comparable across the estate and the second facility costs less effort than the first.

01
Facility walk-through
A guided tour with your engineers to establish the as-is state, room by room and skid by skid, and to document what the drawings no longer say. This is where the twin's geometry and topology are captured.
02
Build asset inventory depth
Fully passive, operations-safe collection to device level: make, model, firmware, protocol and communication pattern. Sampled inventory, vulnerabilities and observed threats — the foundation for monitoring and vulnerability management.
03
Architecture assessment — IT and OT
Review segmentation, conduits, remote access, trust boundaries and management planes across both estates, and record where the design and the installation have diverged.
04
Sector-specific threat model
Threat assessment against the techniques actually used in your sector, mapped to the MITRE frameworks, so scenarios are grounded in observed adversary behaviour rather than generic risk categories.
05
Incident response diagnostic and tabletop
Structured sessions with IT and OT teams together, then a facilitated exercise against a scenario drawn from your own facility — including a ransomware readiness diagnostic.
06
OT forensics readiness
Determine whether the facility can supply the evidence an investigation depends on — logging coverage, retention, time sync, capture points and access — before you need it rather than during an incident.
