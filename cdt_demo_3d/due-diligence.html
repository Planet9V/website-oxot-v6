Decision Sprint FAQ

A Decision Sprint is a fast, consulting-driven engagement focused on a temporary digital twin instance built to answer a specific change, CapEx, M&A, or supplier question. A Twin Build is a comprehensive 30-day program delivering a permanent, living digital twin designed for daily engineering operations and long-term facility change management.
You receive the complete compliance artifacts, executive loss memos, and mathematical proof ledger. The model data is packaged for your records and the active runtime instance is spun down, meaning you never incur unexpected software subscription overhead. Should you decide to upgrade to a persistent living twin later, the baseline model can be immediately reactivated.
Piping and instrumentation diagrams (P&IDs), single-line power schematics (SLDs), network switch configuration backups, controller asset inventories, and third-party vendor maintenance specifications.
