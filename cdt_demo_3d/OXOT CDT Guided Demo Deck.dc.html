ONBOARDING OVERVIEW

See how we onboard a Cyber Digital Twin

Four phases, two workshops — walked through on one worked example: a hyperscale AI datacentre.

Illustrative example — not a live client model

What this walkthrough is

IT IS

A guided tour of the onboarding method, running on an example facility we built ourselves.

Deliberately limited — enough interaction to show what the method feels like, and no more.

IT IS NOT

The Cyber Digital Twin itself, which carries your live asset data, configurations, bills of material and threat intelligence.

That produces the full artifact set, and we show it separately.

Your facility will look nothing like the example. The process is the same.

A hyperscale datacentre, because it is hard

Everything that makes OT risk difficult in one place — and two independent cells, which gives the demo a question worth asking: does a compromise in one cell stay in that cell?

Power

Grid intake through MV switchgear, transformers, UPS, generators and per-cell busway to the rack.

Cooling

Central chillers and towers feeding row CDUs, with direct-to-chip loops into each rack.

IT & network

Carrier demarcs, firewalls, dual spine, per-row distribution, jumpbox and out-of-band console.

OT boundary

Per-cell OT switch and firewall, data diode, protocol gateway, passive monitoring from the NOC.

Life safety

Fire panels, aspirating detection, suppression and the hardwired interlocks that bypass the network.

Physical security

Perimeter detection, mantrap, cameras and door contacts back to the security officer's desk.
