FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Engineering & Governance Drilldown

OXOT ingests your existing engineering artifacts (CAD/PDF P&IDs, electrical single-line diagrams, and switch configurations). Our Seldon Engine extracts node tags, physical clearances, and conduit routings without connecting intrusive agents or sending active network probes to physical controllers.
ALE is computed as Single Loss Expectancy (SLE) × Annualized Rate of Occurrence (ARO). SLE is derived from Level 1 kinetic physical damage models (e.g., thermal equipment burn, production downtime, regulatory fines), while ARO is calibrated via 10,000 Monte Carlo campaign simulations matched against 389 adversary profiles in the TACAM 7D threat matrix.
The platform automatically generates audit-ready compliance dossiers for the European Cyber Resilience Act (CRA Annex VII), IEC 62443-3-2 Zone & Conduit Risk Assessments, NIS2 Directive, and CycloneDX 1.6 5-BOMs (SBOM, HBOM, CBOM, SaaSBOM, and Ops-BOM).
