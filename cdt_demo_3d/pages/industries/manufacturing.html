MANUFACTURING OT RISKS

High-Consequence Facility Risk Scenarios

01 / SAFETY SYSTEM TRIPS

SIS Emergency Shutdown Overrides

Corrupting communications between basic process control systems (BPCS) and Safety Instrumented Systems (SIS) can trigger nuisance shutdowns ($500k+ per restart) or suppress emergency venting.

02 / BATCH RECIPE CORRUPTION

MES to PLC Recipe Tampering

Subtle unauthorized modifications to temperature ramp curves, mix ratios, or sterilization hold times ruin high-value product batches, invalidating FDA 21 CFR Part 11 or GMP compliance records.

03 / OEM ACCESS & ROBOTICS

Robot Cell Maintenance Bypasses

Robotics OEMs maintain direct cellular or unmanaged VPN bridges into welding and packaging cells, bypassing Purdue Level 3.5 firewalls and exposing shopfloor networks to ransomware.

Can we change safely?
