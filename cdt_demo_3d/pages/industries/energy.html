01 / SUBSTATION AUTOMATION

Protection Relay Trip Injection

IEDs communicating via unauthenticated IEC 61850 GOOSE multicast frames are vulnerable to spoofed trip commands, forcing transmission lines offline and cascading load across adjacent substations.

02 / BESS & RENEWABLE STORAGE

Battery Thermal Runaway

Tampering with Battery Management System (BMS) cell balancing, charging rate limits, or HVAC cooling setpoints can cause catastrophic lithium-ion thermal runaway while blinding the central NOC.

03 / REMOTE DISPATCH & DMZ

Transmission Telemetry Loss

DNP3/IP or IEC 60870-5-104 links crossing the operational DMZ allow attackers to corrupt AGC (Automatic Generation Control) setpoints, triggering emergency generation curtailment and grid imbalance penalties.
