WATER OT VULNERABILITIES

Sector-Specific Failure Modes

01 / CHEMICAL OVERDOSING

Disinfection & pH Dosing Skids

Unauthorized tampering with PLC recipe setpoints (e.g. sodium hypochlorite or caustic soda) can inject unsafe chemical concentrations directly into finished water reservoirs before online analyzers alert operators.

02 / DISTRIBUTED LIFT STATIONS

Remote Cellular & Radio RTUs

Wastewater lift stations and remote water towers depend on unencrypted cellular modems or legacy radio links. Compromising an RTU enables attackers to disable lead/lag pumps, creating raw sewage backflows.

03 / INTEGRATOR ACCESS

Shared Third-Party Portals

Small municipal utilities often outsource SCADA maintenance to external system integrators who use persistent remote desktop or VPN connections, creating supply-chain lateral movement paths into critical PLC networks.

DECISION FRAMEWORK

The Four Decisions in Water Utility Operations

Enable municipal directors and facility superintendents to justify prioritized budget requests with defensible public-health and regulatory evidence.

What do we fix first?

Prioritize pathways that directly reach chemical dosing controllers and master lift station telemetry ahead of peripheral office IT.

What should we spend?

Target capital into physical interlocks and protocol isolation appliances rather than massive SCADA overhaul contracts.

Can we change safely?

Simulate PLC logic changes, cellular gateway updates, and firewall rules in the twin to prevent accidental water supply interruptions.

What can we ignore?

A written, defensible rationale to leave isolated flow transmitters alone where mechanical check valves prevent contamination.

Scenario: Water Treatment Facility Sodium Hypochlorite Dosing Loop

Progression from baseline vulnerability to virtual control simulation and certified facility maintenance output.

Integrator Remote Access VPN → Facility SCADA HMI → Chemical Dosing PLC (Modbus TCP) → Overdose Event (45,000 Consumers Affected)
1. Baseline As-Documented SL-1 / EXPOSED

Entry Point: Third-party contractor remote desktop bridge into facility SCADA network.

Lateral Path: Flat Ethernet routing allows write access to Sodium Hypochlorite Dosing PLC without secondary authentication.

Consequence: Attacker ramps dosing rate 10x normal threshold, causing distribution water quality violation and emergency boil-water notice.

2. Proposed Virtual Control VIRTUAL TEST

Simulated Remediation:
• Establish IEC 62443 Zone 2 boundary isolating chemical dosing PLCs.
• Install hardware maximum-flow limit switch on chemical metering pump stroke.
• Enforce unidirectional syslog reporting to central municipal IT.

Simulated Path Result: Software injection bounded by physical mechanical limit switch (0% overdose risk).
