PHYSICAL OT CONSTRAINTS

Where Cyber Threats Cross Into Physical Megawatts

01 / THERMAL RUNAWAY

Cooling Train & CDU Loops

Modern AI dense racks (40kW–120kW/rack) require direct-to-chip liquid cooling. A hijacked BMS or Modbus gateway altering valve positioning causes heat dissipation failure within seconds, triggering emergency compute throttles.

02 / ELECTRICAL SWITCHGEAR

EPMS & Protection Relays

Substation transformers, MV/LV switchgear, and static transfer switches (STS) communicate via IEC 61850 and BACnet. Cyber manipulation of breaker trip thresholds can desynchronize redundant power buses.

03 / BOUNDARY EXPOSURE

Vendor Remote Maintenance

Chiller OEMs, generator vendors, and DCIM providers frequently maintain cellular or VPN bypasses into Level 2 facility networks, bypassing core zero-trust firewalls and introducing third-party supply-chain vectors.
