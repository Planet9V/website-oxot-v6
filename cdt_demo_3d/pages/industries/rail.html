RAIL OPERATIONAL CONSTRAINTS

Passenger Transit & Freight Operational Risks

01 / SIGNALING & INTERLOCKING

Fail-Safe Gridlock Cascades

Interlockings fail to restrictive states (red signals). A denial-of-service on trackside communications forces entire rail corridors into emergency stop, stranding commuter trains and blocking freight hubs.

02 / ROLLING STOCK TCMS

Passenger Wi-Fi to TCMS Bridging

Modern trainsets feature passenger Wi-Fi, CCTV, and passenger information systems sharing underlying Ethernet trains. Inadequate physical or VLAN isolation risks lateral pivoting into brake/door control buses.

03 / FREIGHT PTC & DISPATCH

Movement Authority Spoofing

US freight operators rely on Positive Train Control (PTC) radio dispatch. Corrupting movement authority packets can cause automated emergency brake enforcement, blocking critical supply-chain junctions.
