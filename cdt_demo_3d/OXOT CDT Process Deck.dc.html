Where the outputs go

One dataset, every team. The usual failure of an OT assessment is that each team receives a different document and none of them reconcile.

Executives and risk

Executive briefing and readout. Input to the organisational risk ledger where the sponsor is organisational. Consequence tied to numbers, top down.

Facility managers

SecRAC for the facility. Zone and conduit responsibilities, and the controls that apply to facility they operate day to day.

Safety and reliability

Reliability and safety critical items returned to the hazard log, so the safety team's own artifact stays current.

IT, OT, controls, network, operations, security

The full detailed report aligned to IEC 62443 and CLC/TS 50701, and the model itself. Bottom-up engineering view that earns trust.

THE OUTCOME

A formal, defensible process and artifact set for an IEC 62443 determination of SL-C — including where it is negotiated down to SL-A.

Consequence-driven reporting from the top down, an engineering and operations view from the bottom up, and one common set of information for every team.
