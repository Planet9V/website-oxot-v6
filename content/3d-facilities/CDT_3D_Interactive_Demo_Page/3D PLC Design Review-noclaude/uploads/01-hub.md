## What it is, in one sentence

Ask your facility a question — *"what happens if this pump fails," "what happens if this vendor gets
breached," "what happens if we don't patch this system for six months"* — and get a real,
engineering-grounded answer. Not a guess.

## The mechanic

Most security tools watch your network and tell you when something looks wrong. OXOT does something
different: it builds an actual model of your facility — every asset, every connection, every safety
zone — from your real engineering data. Then you can change that model. Simulate a compromised
device. Simulate a vendor going down. Simulate a patch you haven't applied yet.

The twin shows you the real consequence: which systems are affected, what it would cost, and what
your safety and reliability engineers would actually see happen. That's the difference between an
alert and an answer.

## Why this matters

**Stop guessing where to spend.** See the actual exposure on your specific equipment, not an industry
benchmark.

**Speak one language across teams.** Safety, engineering, IT, and finance can all ask the same model
different questions and get consistent, physically grounded answers.

**Turn compliance into a byproduct, not a project.** Your IEC 62443 zone assignments and target
security levels update as your facility does — not once a year in a spreadsheet.

## Who it's for

Built for teams running physical infrastructure where a cyber incident has a physical consequence —
data centers, manufacturing plants, and critical infrastructure. If your organization has safety
engineers, reliability engineers, control systems, and a security team who don't currently share one
picture of risk, this is built for you.
