# Headline
> Not another alert feed. Not another checklist.

## Subheadline
Most OT security tools do one of two things well: watch your network for anomalies, or check you off
against a compliance standard. OXOT does something neither does — it connects what's exposed to what
it would actually cost your specific facility, so you know what to do about it.

---

## How the categories compare

**Passive monitoring tools** tell you something changed on the network. They're built to detect
anomalies — not to tell you what a given exposure would cost, or whether it touches a safety system.
Useful for detection. Not built for prioritization.

**Compliance / checklist tools** tell you whether you match a standard. They're built to satisfy an
audit — not to reflect what's actually likely to go wrong on your specific plant, with your specific
equipment.

**OXOT's Cyber Digital Twin** starts from your actual facility — assets, connections, safety zones —
and computes real engineering risk and financial exposure from it. It doesn't replace detection or
compliance reporting. It's the layer that turns both into a ranked, defensible answer: what to fix
first, and why.
