# Headline
> One model. Every team asks it something different.

## Subheadline
Today, most facilities run risk decisions on five different spreadsheets that don't talk to each
other. The digital twin is the one place safety, engineering, IT, and finance can all look and get an
answer that agrees with itself.

**Pick your role below to see what you get.**

### Safety
What happens if this fails, and who's exposed? See the real hazard picture behind every asset — not a
generic checklist, the actual failure modes and consequences for your equipment.

### Reliability
Where is the next failure actually coming from? Real reliability figures, tied to the same model your
security team is using — so you're not defending your numbers against a different picture.

### Operations
What does this change actually cost me in downtime? See the operational impact of a security decision
before it happens on the floor, not after.

### IT & Security
What's exposed, and what matters most? Real vulnerability, exploit, and vendor-risk data matched to
your specific assets — ranked by what would actually happen if exploited, not generic severity alone.

### Controls Engineering
Does this touch my interlock logic, or just IT? See exactly which layer of the architecture — process,
control, or IT — a given exposure sits in, so you're not guessing whether it's your problem.

### Finance
What's the dollar exposure, and is it worth spending on? See risk expressed as annualized loss
exposure — a number you can put in a budget conversation, not a severity color.

### Procurement / Supply Chain
Which vendors are actually a risk? Vendor and supplier exposure scored against real vulnerability and
trust data — so a sourcing decision isn't made blind to security posture.

### Support
What's the real root cause, and who needs to know? When something happens, trace it back through the
same model everyone else is using — no separate ticket-only picture of what's connected to what.

### Executives
Where should the next dollar go? One prioritized view of risk across the whole facility — not eleven
dashboards that each tell a different story.

### Managers
What's actually getting fixed, and is it working? Track remediation against real risk reduction, not
just ticket close rates.
