## Summary and Conclusions

Panduit Corp emerges from this research as a well-established, capital-intensive infrastructure vendor with exceptional market penetration (90% Fortune 100), strong innovation capability (3,000+ patents, state-of-the-art R&D), and a vertically integrated business model. Panduit represents a lower-risk vendor choice based on its 70-year history, 90% Fortune 100 penetration, and vertical integration, but the limited public security disclosure creates enterprise customer friction in heavily regulated verticals (finance, government, critical infrastructure). Competitive vendors with formal PSIRT programs and published certifications maintain customer confidence advantage in these markets.
