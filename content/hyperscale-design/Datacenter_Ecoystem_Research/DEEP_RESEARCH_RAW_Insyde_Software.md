### Pricing Model and Revenue Streams

Insyde operates a **B2B licensing and services revenue model** with three primary streams:

1. **Source Code Disclosure Fees**: OEMs pay for access to customizable firmware source code, enabling platform-specific modifications and proprietary integrations
2. **Per-Unit Royalty Fees**: Recurring revenue based on hardware units shipped with Insyde firmware (primary revenue driver for high-volume ODM partners)
3. **Non-Recurring Engineering (NRE) Fees**: Custom development services for specialized implementations, performance optimization, and integration work

**Strategic Insight**: This **mixed recurring + one-time revenue model** creates **predictable baseline revenue** (royalties) combined with **high-margin professional services** (NRE). As OEM volumes grow, **per-unit royalties become increasingly profitable** due to **minimal marginal costs of firmware delivery**. **Scope and Applicability**: The **EU Cyber Resilience Act (2024/2847)** applies to **"products with digital elements"** as defined in Article 3(1). Insyde's firmware products (InsydeH2O, Supervyse, TapRUUT, BlinkBoot) clearly fall within this scope.

**Company Positioning**: Insyde announced at **Embedded World 2026 (March 6, 2026)** that it is **"promoting shift-left firmware design and CRA-readiness"** with emphasis on:
- **CRA compliance foundation across full firmware stack**
- **Shift-left security**: Proactive security integration at design phase (vs. bolted-on security)
- **InsydeH2O UEFI BIOS validated across x86 and Arm architectures** for CRA-ready implementations

**Article 7 Classification (Class I vs. Class II)**: The CRA requires **classification of products as Class I (simpler) or Class II (critical infrastructure)** based on risk assessment:
- **Expected Classification for Insyde**: **Likely Class II for server/datacenter firmware** (given critical infrastructure role), **Class I or II for client/IoT firmware** (depending on specific threat assessment)
- **Status**: Insyde has **not publicly specified Article 7 classification** for its product portfolio; classification likely determined in coordination with OEM customers during product integration

**Compliance Timeline**: CRA applies to **new products placed on market starting January 1, 2026**; existing products have **compliance deadlines starting 2027-2028** depending on product risk classification.
