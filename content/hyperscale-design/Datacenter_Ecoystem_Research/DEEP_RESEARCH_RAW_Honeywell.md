**Scope & Applicability:** The CRA, effective December 11, 2027, applies to all "products with digital elements" placed on the EU market. Honeywell confirmed applicability to its product portfolio [[29]](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cyber-resilience-act) [[30]](https://www.honeywell.com/us/en/supplier/eu-cyber-resilience-act)

**Article 7 — Product Classification:** The CRA establishes two product classes:
