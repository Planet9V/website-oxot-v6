### 5.1 EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) – Article 3(1) and Article 7 Scope

**CRA Applicability to Anybus Products:**
HMS has **explicitly confirmed** that Anybus Communicators and CompactCom products are **"products with digital elements" qualifying for CRA Article 3(1) scope** [[52]](https://www.techmezine.com/industrial/anybus-communicators-cra-ready-industrial-gateways-updated-meet-cyber-resilience-act-requirements/) [[53]](https://www.industryemea.com/news/110972-anybus-communicators-are-cra-ready-industrial-gateways-updated-to-meet-cyber-resilience-act-requirements) [[54]](https://www.usa-automation.net/news/110972-anybus-communicators-are-cra-ready-industrial-gateways-updated-to-meet-cyber-resilience-act-requirements). This means:
- Products contain processing capabilities and digital data storage
- Products connect to the internet (wired or wireless) or are intended for networked connection
- CRA obligations apply to product lifecycle (design through end-of-life)

**CRA Article 7 Classification (Class I vs. Class II):**
The CRA categorizes products with digital elements into three tiers:
- **Class I** (default): Products not meeting Class II criticality criteria
- **Class II** (important): Products meeting specific risk criteria (critical sectors, large-scale deployment, third-party dependency)
- **Critical products** (subset of Class II): Mandated additional security measures

**HMS Stated Classification: NOT SPECIFIED in public materials reviewed**. Based on product function (industrial gateway, not life-critical control system), likely **Class I**, but definitive classification has not been disclosed. Customers requiring CRA compliance documentation should request the official Declaration of Conformity from HMS including Article 7 classification.

**CRA Compliance Timeline & Penalties:**
- **Effective Date**: December 11, 2027 (mandatory compliance date) [[23]](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cyber-resilience-act)
- **Reporting Obligation**: September 11, 2026 (vulnerability disclosure requirements begin)
- **Compliance Penalties**: 
  - Security vulnerability: up to **\$15 million EUR or 2.5% of annual global revenue** (whichever greater) [[46]](https://www.windriver.com/resource/eu-cyber-resilience-act-faq)
  - Inadequate documentation: up to **\$10 million EUR or 2% of annual global revenue**

### 5.3 NERC CIP – North American Bulk Electric System

**HMS Applicability**:

Customers operating NERC CIP-critical infrastructure may impose NERC CIP-aligned supplier requirements (e.g., NERC CIP-005 baseline cybersecurity, vulnerability management) on HMS as a vendor, but this is customer-driven rather than HMS-initiated.

**Recommendation**: For sales to utilities and energy infrastructure operators, explicitly ask whether customer is NERC CIP-regulated (if so, request vendor security questionnaire and compliance documentation).

### 5.4 NRC 10 CFR 73.54 – Nuclear Security

**HMS Applicability**: **No evidence found** of HMS manufacturing nuclear-specific products or seeking NRC certification. Anybus products are general-purpose industrial gateways and are **unlikely to qualify for nuclear security systems** (which require dedicated design, quality assurance per 10 CFR Part 50 Appendix B, and NRC-specific certification). ### 10.2 Vulnerability Assessment by Severity

**HIGH Severity (CVSS 7.0+):**
- **CVE-2024-7755**: CVSS 7.1 (EWON FLEXY 202 – weak credential encoding). Requires network access + user action (clicking link) but has high confidentiality impact. Affects installed EWON product fleet.

**MEDIUM Severity (CVSS 4.0–6.9):**
- **CVE-2024-6558**: CVSS 6.3 (CompactCom 30 XSS). Requires network access + user interaction; medium complexity. Affects legacy CompactCom 30 (succeeded by CompactCom 40).
- **CVE-2025-0432**: CVSS 5.7–6.9 (EWON Flexy 202 cleartext credentials). Requires adjacent network access + user interaction; high confidentiality impact but lower integrity/availability.

**LOW/Unknown Severity:**
- 2024-06-26 Anybus X-Gateway DoS: CVE not specified; severity unknown
- 2024-06-26 Anybus HICP vulnerability: CVE not specified; severity unknown
- 2024-01-12 Intesis DHCP/UDP: CVE not specified; severity unknown
- 2023-05-02 Anybus Wireless BLE: CVE not specified; severity unknown

### 10.3 Patch & Remediation Status

**Critical Limitation**: 
Of the three highest-impact CVEs identified:
- **CVE-2024-6558**: No firmware patch released; remediation is operational (disable web server, add password protection, or replace with CompactCom 40)
- **CVE-2024-7755**: No firmware patch identified; remediation is configuration/architectural (use Talk2M encrypted cloud service instead)
- **CVE-2025-0432**: No firmware patch identified; remediation is operational (use cloud service alternative)

**Implication**: These are likely **end-of-life product vulnerabilities** where HMS prioritizes development on newer product generations (CompactCom 40, Ewon Cosy+ with Talk2M cloud) rather than patching legacy products. For new deployments, **specify CompactCom 40 and latest Ewon firmware** to avoid inherited vulnerabilities.

### 10.4 Vulnerability Trends & Risk Trajectory

**Trend**: Increasing disclosure velocity in 2024–2025 (4 CVEs in last 12 months vs. 1–2 in prior years). Likely reflects:
1. **Increased external security research** interest in industrial gateways (critical infrastructure focus)
2. **Improved disclosure coordination** with CISA and NVD (more advisories published)
3. **Actual increase in findings** (less likely; design quality appears stable)

**Risk Trajectory**: 
- **Legacy products (CompactCom 30, EWON FLEXY 20x, Cosy+)**: Elevated residual risk; end-of-life; no patches expected
- **Current products (CompactCom 40, Ewon with Talk2M cloud, Anybus Communicator CRA-ready)**: Lower risk; security-by-design; active patching expected
- **Future roadmap**: CRA-ready products (spring 2026) will enforce secure updates and compliance documentation, reducing future vulnerability discovery lag
