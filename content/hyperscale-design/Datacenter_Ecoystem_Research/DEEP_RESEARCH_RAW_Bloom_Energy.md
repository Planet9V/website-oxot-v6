## 4. TECHNOLOGY & ARCHITECTURE: OT/IoT & CYBERSECURITY POSTURE

### Embedded Systems & Core Technology

**High-Temperature Electrochemistry Platform:**  
Bloom Energy's proprietary solid oxide technology operates at 700–1,000°C (internal fuel cell combustion), converting fuel (methane, hydrogen, biogas) directly into electricity through non-combustion electrochemical conversion [[37]](https://www.bloomenergy.com/resource/bloom-energy-server/).

**Cell Architecture (3-Layer Ceramic Construction):**

| Layer | Material | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **Anode** | Green nickel oxide-based ink coating | Fuel oxidation |
| **Electrolyte** | Yttria-stabilized zirconia (YSZ) or Scandia-stabilized zirconia (ScSZ) | Oxygen ion conduction |
| **Cathode** | Lanthanum strontium manganite (black ink) | Oxygen reduction |

Each cell produces approximately 25W at rated conditions; industrial systems contain hundreds to thousands of cells in series/parallel configurations to reach 100 kW–200 kW output per module [[9]](https://www.fastcompany.com/1561844/how-does-bloom-box-energy-server-work).

**Electrical Integration (2026 Generation Enhancement):**
New generation Bloom Energy Server designs incorporate **800V DC direct electrical interface**, enabling seamless integration with AI server rack power distribution without conversion losses, a significant advancement for hyperscaler deployment [[35]](https://investor.bloomenergy.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2026/Bloom-Energy-Reports-Record-First-Quarter-2026-Results-and-Raises-Full-Year-2026-Guidance/default.aspx).
