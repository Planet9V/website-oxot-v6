## 4. Technology and Architecture

### OT/IoT Embedded Systems

Kohler Power Systems generators integrate industrial operational technology (OT) systems with IoT connectivity, enabling remote monitoring, predictive analytics, and autonomous failover capabilities. The architecture is typical of modern critical infrastructure backup power systems.

#### Network Protocols Supported

Kohler equipment communicates via multiple industrial-standard protocols:

| Protocol | Transport | Application | Implementation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Modbus RTU** | RS-485 serial (up to 1,200m) | Local area device polling | Slave devices report status/alarms to master controller |
| **Modbus TCP/IP** | Ethernet/TCP-IP | Remote monitoring, cloud integration | Monitor III software, web-based HMI |
| **SNMP (Simple Network Management Protocol)** | TCP/UDP Port 161 | Network device discovery, MIB polling | APM/Decision-Maker controllers expose SNMP MIB objects |
| **BACnet** | IP/serial | Building automation integration | Gateway converters available for legacy BACnet networks [[21]](https://store.chipkin.com/articles/kohler-to-bacnet-and-modbus-protocol-gateways) |
| **LonWorks** | LonTalk protocol | Distributed facility control | Less common; typically via gateway conversion |
| **CAN (Controller Area Network)** | Serial bus | Multi-generator synchronization, real-time control | Proprietary Kohler implementation in Decision-Maker series |
| **J1939** | SAE standard (diesel engines) | Engine diagnostics, fuel consumption, operating hours | Leveraged from Kohler's engine manufacturing heritage |
| **OPC UA (OLE for Process Control)** | TCP/IP | Modern SCADA/MES integration | Emerging capability in next-generation controllers |

## 6. Regulatory Exposure

### EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) – Article 3(1) Scope

The **EU Cyber Resilience Act**, effective December 11, 2027, classifies products subject to cybersecurity requirements as "products with digital elements." [[66]](https://hyperproof.io/understanding-the-relationship-between-nis2-and-the-eu-cyber-resilience-act/) [[70]](https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2025/understanding-key-eu-cybersecurity-legislative-acts-nis2,-cer,-and-cra)

**Analysis of Kohler Applicability:**
- **Trigger:** Any Kohler generator, automatic transfer switch, or control system with embedded digital components (CPU, firmware, network connectivity) exported to EU falls within Article 3(1) "products with digital elements" scope
- **Likely Classification for Kohler Products:** 
  - Decision-Maker controllers with Modbus/SNMP: **SUBJECT** (has network management protocols)
  - APM402/603/802: **SUBJECT** (embedded digital control)
  - Monitor III software: **SUBJECT** (software product with digital elements)
  - Basic mechanical generator units without controls: **POTENTIALLY EXEMPT** (no significant digital elements)

### NIS2 Directive (EU Network and Information Security Directive 2)

**Applicability Trigger:** NIS2 applies to "essential entities" in 11 critical sectors (energy, transport, water, healthcare, waste, digital infrastructure, public administration, space, financial services, manufacturing, chemicals) [[68]](https://www.openkritis.de/eu/eu-nis-2-rce-directive.html) [[70]](https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2025/understanding-key-eu-cybersecurity-legislative-acts-nis2,-cer,-and-cra) [[72]](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/nis2-directive)

**Kohler's Regulatory Position:** Kohler Power is **not itself an essential entity**; however, its customers are:
- **Direct applicability:** Kohler sales to EU utility operators, grid operators, and large data centers (classified as essential entities under NIS2) create **supply chain exposure**
- **Customer obligations:** EU essential entities must ensure supply chain cybersecurity; Kohler may face customer-driven security audits and certifications
- **Kohler's indirect obligations:** Likely non-binding but significant—customers will demand evidence of secure product development, vulnerability disclosure processes, and SBOM transparency

**Compliance Deadline:** October 2026 (imminent as of June 2026 report date); Kohler products must support customer NIS2 compliance even if Kohler itself is not directly regulated.

### NERC CIP (North American Electric Reliability Corporation Critical Infrastructure Protection)

**Applicability Trigger:** NERC CIP applies to equipment connected to the Bulk Electric System (BES), which includes:

- **Indirect applicability:** Data center generators serving hyperscalers typically operate in island mode (not grid-connected) and do not trigger NERC CIP
- **Standards affected (if applicable):**
