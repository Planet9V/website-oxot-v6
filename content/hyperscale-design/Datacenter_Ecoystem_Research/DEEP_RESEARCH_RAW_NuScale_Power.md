## Executive Summary

NuScale Power Corporation (NYSE: SMR) is a US-based designer and technology provider of small modular reactors (SMRs) currently in advanced regulatory approval and pre-commercial deployment stages. As of June 2026, the company operates as a **pre-revenue technology company** with no deployed operating reactors, meaning comprehensive financial and operational security disclosures remain limited. The organization has achieved significant regulatory validation—with the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) approving its **77 MWe uprated design in May 2025** and its 6-module VOYGR configuration design approval as well—but continues to burn approximately **\$50-100 million annually** in R&D and operational expenses while pursuing commercialization partnerships across the United States, Romania, Ghana, and Poland.

## Conclusion: Strategic Implications for B2B Sales Research

NuScale Power Corporation presents a **unique market position as the first and only NRC-certified SMR technology provider, but operates in an extremely long sales cycle (7-10 years) with all customers in pre-commercial feasibility stages.** The organization demonstrates strong regulatory credibility (NRC design approval), substantial international backing (Korean manufacturing partners, \$600M+ Fluor investment), and diversified application potential (electricity, desalination, hydrogen, industrial heat). However, critical factors constrain near-term revenue:

1. **Pre-Commercial Stage:** Zero deployed operating reactors; all financial metrics reflect R&D spending, not power generation revenue
2. **Capital Intensity & Extended Timelines:** Customer projects span 7-10 years from FEED to operation; investors face extended capital deployment
3. **Supply Chain Concentration:** Heavy reliance on South Korean manufacturers creates geopolitical risk and limits manufacturing flexibility
4. **Financial Pressure:** Company burning \$50-100M annually; securities litigation and stock decline (78% from peak) raise investor confidence concerns
5. **Cybersecurity Transparency Gap:** Absence of documented PSIRT, published security policies, and CVE disclosures creates uncertainty about security maturity (may reflect pre-operational status or limited disclosure culture)
6. **Regulatory Execution Risk:** Final NRC operating licenses, utility cost-benefit approval, and regulatory timelines all remain unresolved

For B2B sales teams, NuScale represents a **strategic long-term customer opportunity** for companies providing:
- **Regulatory compliance and documentation services** (validated by Certrec partnership)
- **Cybersecurity frameworks and 10 CFR 73.54 compliance** (market entry as NRC license issuance approaches)
- **Supply chain financing and component manufacturing** (Korean manufacturing partnerships mature)
- **Project management and FEED execution** (downstream after design approval)
- **Power plant operations and maintenance services** (after ~2029-2030 when first plants approach commercialization)

The absence of reference customers and extensive legal/financial risks require risk-adjusted sales strategies prioritizing government/utility accounts and emphasizing NuScale's NRC regulatory advantages.
