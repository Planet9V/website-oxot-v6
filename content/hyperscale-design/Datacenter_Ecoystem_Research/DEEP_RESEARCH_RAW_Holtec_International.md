### Small Modular Reactor (SMR) Products — Core Growth Drivers

#### SMR-300 (Advanced Model)

**The SMR-300 is a 300 MWe pressurized water reactor (PWR) designed with passive Engineered Safety Features (ESF) and walk-away safety philosophy**, requiring no operator intervention during postulated accidents [[78]](https://holtecinternational.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/HTB-085-SMR-300-Rev-2.pdf) [[79]](https://holtecinternational.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/HTB-085-SMR-300-Rev-5.pdf) [[80]](https://holtecinternational.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/HTB-085-SMR-300-Rev-4.pdf). Key specifications:

- **Net electric output:** 300 MWe per unit in a dual-unit plant configuration (640+ MWe total)
- **Configuration:** Dual-unit plant occupies approximately **30 acres of land** (half the footprint of conventional large reactors) [[79]](https://holtecinternational.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/HTB-085-SMR-300-Rev-5.pdf) [[80]](https://holtecinternational.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/HTB-085-SMR-300-Rev-4.pdf)
- **Design life:** 80-year operational lifetime [[78]](https://holtecinternational.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/HTB-085-SMR-300-Rev-2.pdf)
- **Passive safety features:** 
  - Passive Core Makeup (PCM) system (no active pumps required)
  - Passive Containment Heat (PCH) removal (gravity-driven)
  - Annular water reservoir ultimate heat sink [[83]](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML2409/ML24092A413.pdf) [[84]](https://gda.holtecbritain.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/PSR-Part-A-Chapter-2-General-Design-Aspects-and-Site-Characteristics.pdf)
- **Emergency planning zone:** No exclusion zone required due to passive safety [[78]](https://holtecinternational.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/HTB-085-SMR-300-Rev-2.pdf)
- **Load-following capability:** Can be configured for load-following, but Holtec's integrated **Green Boiler thermal energy storage system enables constant base-load operation with 5× longer lifecycle than lithium-ion batteries** [[79]](https://holtecinternational.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/HTB-085-SMR-300-Rev-5.pdf) [[82]](https://holtecinternational.com/company/divisions/holtec-asia/smr-300/)
- **Thermal integration:** **HI-KOOL air-cooled condenser technology** replaces traditional water-cooled systems, reducing environmental impact [[85]](https://www.ans.org/news/article-2719/overview-of-holtecs-smr160/)
- **Target deployment:** **Palisades Nuclear Generating Station restart, targeted for 2030** (first decommissioned U.S. nuclear plant to restart) [[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holtec_International)

**Safety analysis:** The SMR-300 design uses **advanced computer codes (MAAP, GOTHIC, SCALE, ARCON96, RADTRAD) and conforms to ASME, ANSI, ANS, ACI, AISC, ASCE, IEC, and IEEE standards** [[83]](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML2409/ML24092A413.pdf) [[84]](https://gda.holtecbritain.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/PSR-Part-A-Chapter-2-General-Design-Aspects-and-Site-Characteristics.pdf).

Holtec International is a **privately held, founder-controlled nuclear equipment supplier transitioning from a mature, high-margin storage business into high-growth small modular reactor commercialization.** The company commands unmatched market dominance in spent fuel storage systems, backed by \$1.5 billion in DOE financing and a strategic partnership with South Korea's Hyundai Engineering & Construction.

**Key intelligence points for sales professionals:**
- **Revenue:** ~\$500M–\$1.4B (conflicting estimates; private status limits transparency)
- **Leadership:** Dr. Kris Singh (CEO/Founder, 40 years); Kelly Trice (President, since 2024); Thomas P. Howe (CFO)
- **Strategic focus:** SMR-300 (300 MWe), SMR-160 (160 MWe), nuclear decommissioning, spent fuel storage
- **Global presence:** 9 countries with divisions in United States, Europe, Asia, and Ukraine
- **Employees:** 501–1,000 (with 18% annual growth)
- **Primary customers:** 118 U.S. nuclear plants; Exelon, Entergy, Energoatom (Ukraine)
- **Key partners:** Hyundai E&C (SMR construction), SNC-Lavalin (decommissioning), Balfour Beatty/Mott MacDonald (UK programs)
- **Security posture:** Zero disclosed CVEs (36 months); NRC 10 CFR 73.54 compliant; no public cybersecurity certifications found
- **Regulatory exposure:** NRC authority (primary), EU CRA (compliance deadline September 2026), GDPR (European operations), Ukraine sanctions risk
- **IPO status:** Preparing IPO with \$10B+ valuation target; not yet public

**Critical gaps for due diligence:** Financial depth (3-year trends), R&D spending, cybersecurity certifications, CRA product classifications, SBOM status, detailed PSIRT structure, and product pricing.
