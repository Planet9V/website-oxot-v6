## 5. Regulatory Exposure

### EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) – Article 3(1) Scope

The **EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) entered into force on December 10, 2024**, with main compliance obligations applying from **December 11, 2027** [[47]](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cyber-resilience-act) [[48]](https://www.bsi.bund.de/EN/Themen/Unternehmen-und-Organisationen/Informationen-und-Empfehlungen/Cyber_Resilience_Act/cyber_resilience_act_node.html). Reporting obligations commence **September 11, 2026** [[47]](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cyber-resilience-act).

**Article 3(1) Scope Definition**: The CRA applies to "**products with digital elements**" sold in the EU market. This broad definition includes low-cost consumer products, business-to-business software, IoT devices, and embedded systems [[48]](https://www.bsi.bund.de/EN/Themen/Unternehmen-und-Organisationen/Informationen-und-Empfehlungen/Cyber_Resilience_Act/cyber_resilience_act_node.html).

**SPX Compliance Status**: **Unknown and not publicly disclosed.** SPX has not published documentation specifying which product lines fall within CRA scope as "products with digital elements." The company's primary products—cooling towers, boilers, locators, and navigation lights—are largely mechanical or electromechanical devices. However, the company's recent **Ingénia Technologies acquisition** (custom air handling units) and acquisitions of software-centric businesses (Sensors & Software Inc., ULC Robotics, Genfare transportation systems) may introduce software-heavy products that qualify as "digital elements."

**Key CRA Compliance Requirements** (applicable to in-scope products) [[47]](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cyber-resilience-act) [[48]](https://www.bsi.bund.de/EN/Themen/Unternehmen-und-Organisationen/Informationen-und-Empfehlungen/Cyber_Resilience_Act/cyber_resilience_act_node.html) [[49]](https://www.wirtek.com/blog/cyber-resilience-act-explained-for-software-and-iot-companies):

## Conclusion

**SPX Technologies, Inc.** is a well-capitalized, publicly traded industrial equipment supplier with strong financial momentum and clear strategic focus on high-growth data center cooling infrastructure. The company's **\$11.4B market capitalization, \$1.98B FY2024 revenue (13.9% growth), and \$700M capacity investment program** position it as a critical supplier to hyperscale cloud infrastructure buildout.

However, the company faces **significant organizational intelligence gaps** in cybersecurity, regulatory compliance, and technology transparency domains. The absence of publicly disclosed CVEs, PSIRT function, and cybersecurity certifications does not indicate security strength—rather, it reflects the company's non-IoT product composition and potential under-investment in security infrastructure transparency.

For B2B customers evaluating SPX as a vendor, **due diligence should focus on direct engagement on cybersecurity certifications, regulatory compliance status under EU CRA and NIS2 Directive, and integration timelines for recently acquired software-centric businesses**. The company's proven acquisition execution and facility investment track record suggest strong operational capability, but security and compliance posture require explicit confirmation through direct vendor engagement rather than relying on public disclosures.
