## 4. TECHNOLOGY AND ARCHITECTURE

### Core Nuclear Technology

**Reactor Design:**
- **Pool-Type Sodium-Cooled Fast Reactor:** Passive cooling via natural convection in a large sodium pool; no external circulation pumps required in the primary loop [[35]](https://www.nrc.gov/reactors/new-reactors/large-lwr/col/aurora-oklo)[[64]]
- **Metallic Fuel Form:** Uranium-zirconium alloy (HALEU) provides high power density and burnup capability; benefits from 30+ years of EBR-II operational data [[32]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aurora_nuclear_reactor)[[64]]
- **Inherent Safety:** Negative temperature reactivity coefficient (reactor self-regulates temperature without active control) and minimal structural damage risk from primary-loop transients [[35]](https://www.nrc.gov/reactors/new-reactors/large-lwr/col/aurora-oklo)[[64]]

**Key Safety Features (Aurora Powerhouse Principal Design Criteria):**
- Large pool structure containing all primary-loop equipment, precluding rapid coolant loss [[37]](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML2522/ML25220A128.pdf)
- Heat pipe decay heat removal (passive, no active systems required post-shutdown) [[32]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aurora_nuclear_reactor)
- No pressurized components in core region, eliminating high-pressure failure modes [[37]](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML2522/ML25220A128.pdf)
- Radionuclide containment achievable without engineered cooling, relying on natural heat dissipation [[37]](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML2522/ML25220A128.pdf)

- ABB-equipped digital operations center commissioned August 2025 [[33]](https://www.neimagazine.com/news/oklo-commissions-aurora-control-room/)

**Applicability:** Questionable. NIS2 (Directive 2022/2555/EU) applies to operators of critical infrastructure (including energy sector entities) within EU member states. Oklo, as a US company, would only trigger NIS2 obligations if operating nuclear facilities within the EU.

**Current Status:** All Aurora projects are planned for US deployment (Indiana, Idaho, Louisiana, Texas, etc.). No EU-based nuclear deployment announced; thus NIS2 obligations do not currently apply.

### NERC CIP (North American Reliability Entity Critical Infrastructure Protection)

**Applicability:** Potentially applicable if Aurora powerhouses become grid-connected or operate as part of NERC-regulated bulk electric systems. Oklo's distributed customer-owned model (selling directly to hyperscalers, industrial users, off-grid) may avoid traditional NERC CIP scope (which applies to transmission operators and balancing authorities).

**Uncertainty:** Oklo's regulatory strategy positions Aurora as customer-owned, on-site generation, which would exempt it from NERC CIP. However, if future deployments interact with grid distribution, NERC compliance assessment may become necessary. No public regulatory filing on NERC CIP applicability identified.

### NRC 10 CFR 73.54 Cybersecurity

**Applicability:** **Confirmed applicable.** NRC's digital asset protection rule (10 CFR 73.54) governs cybersecurity for all NRC-licensed nuclear facilities and must-carry digital assets [[23]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849056/000162828025014490/0001628280-25-014490.txt)[[49]](https://www.highperformr.ai/company/oklo)[[54]][[94]]

**No public patch advisories or vulnerability disclosures identified.** As a pre-commercial entity, Oklo has not released software, firmware, or digital products to the commercial market that would require coordinated vulnerability disclosure and patching.

**NRC Cybersecurity Oversight:** Any vulnerabilities in systems proposed for Aurora will be evaluated by the NRC as part of pre-licensing and operational oversight. Oklo must establish processes for:
- Identifying vulnerabilities in proposed digital assets
- Remediating and verifying fixes
- Coordinating disclosure and remediation with NRC (potentially classified due to security sensitivity)
