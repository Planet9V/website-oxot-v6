### NIS2 Directive (EU Network & Information Systems Directive 2) – Essential Services Applicability

**Regulatory Framework [[31]](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/nis2-directive) [[32]](https://www.insideprivacy.com/cybersecurity-2/new-eu-cyber-law-nis2-enters-into-force/) [[33]](https://www.sentinelone.com/cybersecurity-101/cybersecurity/what-is-nis2/) [[30]](https://iapp.org/news/a/navigating-the-new-eu-cybersecurity-standards-the-nis2-directive-and-cyber-resilience-act):**
- **Effective Date:** January 16, 2023 (in force); October 2026 (compliance deadline for essential entities)
- **Scope:** 18 critical sectors (energy, transport, finance, healthcare, water, waste, digital, space, manufacturing, food chain, health, public administration, governance, etc.)
- **Penalties:** €7 million – €10 million or 1.4% – 2% global annual revenue (essential entities face stricter penalties than important entities)

**IEI Regulatory Status:**
- **Size Threshold:** IEI meets NIS2 coverage trigger (501–1,000 employees, **exceeds €10M annual revenue threshold**)
- **Sector Classification:** As an industrial computing manufacturer, IEI likely qualifies as **"important entity"** under manufacturing sector (unless IEI itself operates critical infrastructure IT systems, which would trigger essential classification)
- **Applicability to IEI Products:** NIS2 applies to **customers who operate IEI products within essential/important entities**, not directly to IEI product manufacturer (except where IEI provides managed services)

**Compliance Implications [[33]](https://www.sentinelone.com/cybersecurity-101/cybersecurity/what-is-nis2/):**
- **For IEI as entity:** Likely subject to NIS2 if IEI operates IT systems supporting its own manufacturing (probable for ERP, SCADA, supply chain systems)
- **For IEI customers:** Customers deploying IEI products in energy, healthcare, transport sectors must ensure products support NIS2 requirements:
  - Risk assessments
  - Incident response procedures
  - Supply chain security controls
  - Vulnerability management

**Compliance Deadline:** October 2026 for essential entities; October 2027 for important entities

### Maturity Gaps & Opportunities

1. **New Security Infrastructure:** PSIRT established November 2024 (less than 6 months operational at date of this report). Limited public CVE disclosure history means security practices remain largely untested in customer deployments.

2. **IEC 62443 Certification Status Unknown:** IEI holds ISO 27001 (IT security) but **specific IEC 62443 certifications (OT security) status not documented**. This creates a potential gap for customers requiring industrial automation cybersecurity certifications.

3. **SBOM Not Disclosed:** Software Bill of Materials availability not mentioned in product documentation. EU CRA compliance (December 2027 deadline) will likely require SBOM publication; proactive disclosure could be a competitive advantage.

4. **Limited Vulnerability Disclosure History:** As a newly formed PSIRT, IEI has not yet published specific CVE advisories. Early performance in this area will establish customer confidence.

### Regulatory Compliance Readiness Timeline

**Near-Term (2026):**
- **October 2026:** NIS2 Directive compliance deadline for "important" entities (IEI likely qualifies if operating manufacturing/IT infrastructure)
- **Immediate Action:** Conduct NIS2 applicability assessment; implement risk assessment and incident response procedures

**Medium-Term (2027):**
- **December 2027:** EU Cyber Resilience Act compliance deadline; all Class I and Class II products must have vulnerability disclosure policies, security updates, and incident reporting procedures in place
- **Action:** Publish SBOM for all products; establish security update supply chain; test incident notification processes

**Key Unknowns:**
- Whether IEI will pursue formal IEC 62443 certification (would strengthen OT market positioning)
- Publication timeline for SBOM and software security documentation
