## 4. TECHNOLOGY & ARCHITECTURE

**Core Cooling Technology**

GRC employs **single-phase immersion cooling**, in which servers are directly submerged in a non-conductive dielectric fluid ( [[6]](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/green_revolution_cooling) [[1]](https://www.datacenterknowledge.com/cooling/green-revolution-s-immersion-cooling-in-action)). This approach differs from two-phase systems (where coolant evaporates/condenses) by maintaining the fluid in liquid state throughout the thermal cycle, simplifying pump design, eliminating condensation management, and reducing system complexity [[6]](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/green_revolution_cooling).

**Server Compatibility & Integration**

GRC's systems accept any standard server with minor modifications:
- **Fan removal** (passive convection within fluid replaces active air cooling)
- **Protective coating** on electronics to prevent fluid interaction with sensitive components
- **Validated OEM configurations:** Dell (via UNICOM Engineering), Cisco (via engineering alliance), Supermicro [[21]](https://manufacturing-today.com/news/could-grcs-immersion-cooling-solutions-be-the-answer-to-the-data-center-energy-dilemma/)

UNICOM Engineering specifically integrates GRC cooling into Dell server variants including C6520-IR, C6620-IR, R650-IR, R660-IR, R750-IR, R750XA-IR, R760-IR, and R760XA-IR with embedded NVIDIA and Intel processors [[17]](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/03/10/3253030/0/en/UNICOM-Engineering-and-GRC-Launch-Fully-Integrated-Immersion-Cooled-AI-Solutions-to-Simplify-Evaluation-Deployment-and-Industrial-Scale-Adoption.html).

**Power & Water Infrastructure Requirements**

- **Electrical:** Dual feeds, 200-240 VAC or 380-480 VAC three-phase, maximum CDU draw 2.3 kW [[33]](https://www.endustriyelenerji.com.tr/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/GRC_Green_Revolution_Cooling_Data_Sheet_ICEraQ_Quad-Duo-One.pdf)
- **Cooling Water:** 50-150 gallons per minute flow rate, inlet temperature 37-122°F (\~3-50°C), supporting both chillerfree (ambient cooling) and chilled-water (data center cooling loop) configurations [[33]](https://www.endustriyelenerji.com.tr/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/GRC_Green_Revolution_Cooling_Data_Sheet_ICEraQ_Quad-Duo-One.pdf)
