## 4. TECHNOLOGY AND ARCHITECTURE

### Operational Technology and Control Systems

**Mark VIe Distributed Control System:** The core operational technology platform for GE Vernova gas, steam, hydro, and nuclear turbines. Mark VIe is a modular DCS designed for incremental technology upgrades without full system replacement; supports diverse applications (combined cycle, simple cycle, nuclear, hydro) and integrates cybersecurity management at architecture level. [[65]](https://www.gevernova.com/gas-power/resources) The system enables remote monitoring, predictive maintenance scheduling, and operational optimization through integrated data analytics.

**Mark VIe Virtual Controller:** Emerging capability enabling asset monitoring and optimization via cloud-connected digital twin representation, supporting cross-asset fleet optimization for utility operators managing portfolios of GE-supplied equipment.

**Grid Software Platform Integration:** Proficy suite integrates with external systems via LDAP (Lightweight Directory Access Protocol) for identity management, RADIUS for centralized authentication, and SYSLOG for security event logging. [[21]](https://www.gevernova.com/grid-solutions/service/cybersecurity) Integration pathways support standard OPC (OLE for Process Control) for real-time data, Modbus/TCP for legacy equipment, and RESTful APIs for modern cloud integration.
