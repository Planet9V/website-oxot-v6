## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Plug Power Inc.** (NASDAQ: PLUG) is a **Delaware-incorporated hydrogen fuel cell and electrolyzer manufacturer** headquartered in **Slingerlands, New York**, with **2,344 full-time employees** as of June 2026 and operations spanning North America, Europe (EMEA), Australia, and international markets. The company operates as a **vertically integrated end-to-end hydrogen platform** spanning production, liquefaction, storage, delivery infrastructure, fueling systems, and stationary/mobile fuel cell power generation.

**Financial Position (FY2024):** Revenue declined **29.4% YoY to \$628.814 million** from FY2023's \$891.340 million, driven by market headwinds and \$949.3 million in asset impairment charges. Gross margin remained severely negative at **−99.4%** (gross loss of \$624.887 million). The company posted a **\$2.105 billion net loss** against an accumulated deficit of \$6.594 billion, with **negative \$728.6 million operating cash flow**. However, **Q1 2026 results show recovery trajectory**: 22% YoY revenue growth with gross margin improving 42 percentage points to −13% from −55% in Q1 2025. Management targets **positive EBITDA by Q4 2026**.

**Market Position:** Plug Power serves **hyperscalers (Amazon, Walmart, Home Depot, FedEx), industrial manufacturers (BMW, Stellantis), and emerging green hydrogen infrastructure customers (Galp, Iberdrola, BP, Hy2gen)**. The company maintains **\$8 billion+ project pipeline** in electrolyzer deployments and controls **2,500 MW annual electrolyzer manufacturing capacity** at its Woodbine, Georgia facility.

**Leadership Transition:** **Jose Luis Crespo** became CEO effective March 2, 2026, after 12+ years at Plug (previously Chief Revenue Officer and President). **Andy Marsh** transitioned to Executive Chairman. The company has undergone significant organizational restructuring under "Project Quantum Leap," targeting operational efficiency and margin expansion.

**Regulatory & Security Profile:** No publicly disclosed cybersecurity incidents, CVEs, or security certifications found in 36-month lookback. **No PSIRT function or Chief Information Security Officer identified** in public disclosures. Regulatory applicability to EU Cyber Resilience Act and NIS2 Directive remains undisclosed.
