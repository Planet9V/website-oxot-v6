## 1. Company Overview and Organizational Foundation

### Legal Structure and Founding Heritage

Everllence SE operates as a **subsidiary of Volkswagen Group**, with complete ownership established through the October 2018 acquisition of MAN Diesel & Turbo by Volkswagen. [[2]](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/man-energy-solutions) [[24]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everllence) The company traces its heritage to a **250+ year legacy** in heavy engineering and propulsion technology. [[2]](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/man-energy-solutions) The modern entity was formed in 2010 through the merger of MAN Diesel & Turbo operations, which consolidated decades of engineering capability. [[24]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everllence)

On **June 4, 2025**, the company officially rebranded from **MAN Energy Solutions SE** to **Everllence SE**—a portmanteau of "ever" and "excellence"—reflecting a strategic pivot toward sustainable energy and decarbonization solutions. [[64]](https://safety4sea.com/man-energy-solutions-changes-name-to-everllence/) The rebranding represents a strategic repositioning from a traditional engine manufacturer to a comprehensive energy solutions provider capable of addressing the global energy transition.
