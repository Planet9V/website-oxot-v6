### EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) — Article 3(1) Scope Assessment

**CRA Effective Date:** December 10, 2024 (entered into force) [[47]](https://hyperproof.io/understanding-the-relationship-between-nis2-and-the-eu-cyber-resilience-act/)[[98]]  
**Compliance Deadline:** December 11, 2027 (main obligations take effect) [[47]](https://hyperproof.io/understanding-the-relationship-between-nis2-and-the-eu-cyber-resilience-act/)[[98]]

**Scope: "Products with Digital Elements"**
CRA applies to products with digital elements that can be connected to networks or have cybersecurity functionality [[49]](https://complycra.eu/cyber-resilience-act-cra-vs-nis2-similarities-and-differences/)[[96]][[98]].

**Carrier Products Likely Within CRA Scope (Assessment based on regulatory definitions, not official Carrier statement):**
- **i-Vu Building Automation Suite** (cloud-connected, network-based, digital controls) ✓
- **WebCTRL Building Automation System** (network-connected, cybersecurity-critical OT) ✓
- **Abound Solutions Suite** (AI-driven digital platform, network connectivity) ✓
- **Network controllers, wireless sensors, smart thermostats** (digital elements, network connectivity) ✓
- **QuantumLeap Thermal Management Systems** (data center controls, network-connected) ✓

**Unlikely in CRA Scope:**
- Passive chillers, compressors, heat exchangers without digital elements ✗
- Traditional non-networked HVAC equipment ✗

**CRA Article 7: Product Classification**
- **Class I (Lower Risk):** Products with limited security impact
- **Class II (Higher Risk):** Products critical to essential services or with significant security exposure
- **Carrier Assessment:** Building automation systems (i-Vu, WebCTRL) likely **Class II** due to criticality to building operations, potential safety implications, and networked nature [[52]](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cyber-resilience-act)[[99]][[101]].

**Key CRA Obligations (Effective Dec 11, 2027):**
- Secure-by-design and secure-by-default requirements
- Minimum 5-year security update commitment
- Coordinated vulnerability disclosure (CVD) program [[47]](https://hyperproof.io/understanding-the-relationship-between-nis2-and-the-eu-cyber-resilience-act/)[[94]][[95]]
- Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) provision
- CE marking and conformity assessment [[52]](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cyber-resilience-act)

**Carrier Status:** Actively preparing for CRA compliance; IEC 62443-4-1 certification demonstrates commitment to secure development (foundational requirement for CRA [[47]](https://hyperproof.io/understanding-the-relationship-between-nis2-and-the-eu-cyber-resilience-act/)[[94]]).
