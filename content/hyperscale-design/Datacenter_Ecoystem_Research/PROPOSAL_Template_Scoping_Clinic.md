## What the Session Covers

*[This is the body of the email or brief proposal — plain language, 150 words max. Pick the right version below and edit.]*

**Version A — Datacenter Operator:**

The 2-hour session maps your facility OT network against IEC 62443 zone and conduit requirements. Bring any network diagram or equipment list you have — a rough sketch is fine. We work through your BMS, PDU management, cooling controls, and any ICS systems live, scoring each zone against SL-2 Foundational Requirements.

You leave with: a 1-page OT zone heat map (Red/Amber/Green per zone), your top 3 gap findings with consequence descriptions, and three clearly scoped next-step options — ranging from no further engagement to a full brownfield assessment.

The heat map is specific enough to take to your VP or your insurer. It is not a formal IEC 62443 assessment — it is the decision tool that tells you whether one is warranted.

**Version B — Product Vendor:**

The 2-hour session maps your product architecture against IEC 62443-4-2 FR1–FR7 at SL-2. Bring your product spec sheet, network interface documentation, and any existing security review materials. We work through each Foundational Requirement live and score where you are against SL-2 requirements.

You leave with: a 1-page FR1–FR7 posture snapshot (Red/Amber/Green per FR), your top 3 gap findings with specific remediation actions, and three clearly scoped next-step options — from self-managed remediation to a full IEC 62443-4-2 gap assessment for CB submission.

This session tells you how far from SL-2 you are and where to focus first — before committing to a 6–12 week formal assessment.
