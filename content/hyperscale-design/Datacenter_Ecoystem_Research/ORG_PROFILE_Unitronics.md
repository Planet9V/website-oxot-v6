**Security Program Profile:** Unitronics does not hold formal third-party cybersecurity certifications such as IEC 62443, ISO 27001, or SOC 2. The company publishes general security guidance recommending network isolation and default credential changes, but the lack of formal certifications represents a vendor risk for enterprise buyers.

### 3.2 EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) Exposure
All Unitronics programmable controllers and software qualify as products with digital elements under CRA Article 3(1):
*   **UniStream & Vision PLCs:** Class II (Important) products due to their role in control systems and critical utility functions.
*   **UniLogic Software & UniCloud:** Subject to strict security audit and self-assessment criteria.

**Penalty Exposure:** Non-compliance with CRA rules exposes Unitronics to administrative fines up to 2.5% of global annual turnover, representing a maximum potential fine of approximately ₪4.8 million.

### 3.3 NIS2 Supply Chain Obligations
The NIS2 Directive affects Unitronics through its European customers in critical sectors (water, energy):
*   **Secure Procurement:** Essential entities must audit Unitronics' secure development and vulnerability management processes.
*   **Vulnerability Disclosure:** Supply chain requirements force Unitronics to coordinate disclosures under EU frameworks.

### 3.4 Publicly Disclosed Vulnerabilities (Last 24 Months)

#### UniStream Controllers (April 2024)
*   **CVE-2024-27767 (CVSS: 8.8):** Authentication bypass vulnerability in UniStream programming interfaces.
*   **CVE-2024-27768 (CVSS: 8.8):** Authorization flaw allowing remote code execution via configuration uploads.

#### Vision Series (April 2024)
*   **CVE-2024-1480 (CVSS: 7.5):** Hardcoded credential vulnerability in legacy Vision controllers. This series was exploited by cyber threat actors in late 2023 at municipal water treatment plants due to default password usage.
