### PSIRT (Product Security Incident Response Team) Structure

**Formal Vulnerability Management Process:** [[58]](https://www.moxa.com/Moxa/media/Support/securityadvisory/Moxa-Vulnerability-Management-Policy_EN.pdf?ext=.pdf)

Moxa operates a **5-stage vulnerability management lifecycle:**

1. **Response** — Initial vulnerability report received via encrypted email (psirt@moxa.com)
2. **Triage** — Assessment of severity using CVSS (Common Vulnerability Scoring System) and internal risk model
3. **Investigation** — Root cause analysis and scope determination across product lines
