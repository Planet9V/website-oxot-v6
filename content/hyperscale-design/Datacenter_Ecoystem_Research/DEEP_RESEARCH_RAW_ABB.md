### 4.1 Network Communication Protocols (AC 800M Control System)

- **Modbus RTU:** Master-only implementation over RS-232C/RS-485, supporting maximum distances of 15 meters (RS-232C) or 1,200 meters (RS-485); implements Function Codes 1–8 and 15–16 for discrete/holding register access [[91, p.85-88]]

## 8. VALUE CHAIN & CHANNEL PARTNERS

### 8.1 OEM Partnership Models

ABB's Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) partnership program operates across four distinct commercial models [[26]](https://new.abb.com/channel-partners/business/original-equipment-manufacturers-oem):

**Supply & Support Agreement:** ABB provides products under ABB branding with ABB type testing and quality assurance. OEM integrates components into their own equipment designs, leveraging ABB's product certification and support resources.

**Channel Cooperation Agreement:** Commercial partnership for specific market segments or applications. OEM resells ABB products under ABB brand through their distribution channels and application expertise.

**OEM License Cooperation Agreement:** Technology transfer arrangement where OEM manufactures ABB-designed products using their own corporate brand, subject to repeated type testing and regulatory compliance [[26]](https://new.abb.com/channel-partners/business/original-equipment-manufacturers-oem).

**Technical Cooperation Agreement:** OEM produces ABB-designed products under their own brand and logo, with each production run subject to renewed type testing to ensure design fidelity [[26]](https://new.abb.com/channel-partners/business/original-equipment-manufacturers-oem).

ABB Ltd. presents a mature, globally diversified industrial technology company with strong market positioning in electrification and automation serving critical infrastructure, manufacturing, and hyperscale data center customers.
