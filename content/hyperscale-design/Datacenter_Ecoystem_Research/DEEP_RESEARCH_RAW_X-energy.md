## Executive Summary

X-Energy, Inc. (NASDAQ: XE) is an advanced nuclear reactor and fuel fabrication company that completed its initial public offering on April 24, 2026, at \$23 per share, raising \$1.02 billion in gross proceeds [[1]](https://rockvilleredi.org/rockville-based-x-energy-files-for-nasdaq-ipo-as-advanced-nuclear-sector-accelerates/). The organization achieved FY2024 revenue of \$83.99 million but operates with negative gross margins and substantial operating losses, sustained by \$944 million in liquid capital and cost-sharing agreements with the U.S. Department of Energy. X-Energy operates across six continents with 1,800+ employees and maintains a vertically integrated business spanning advanced reactor design (Xe-100), high-assay low-enriched uranium (HALEU) fuel fabrication (TRISO-X), and integrated plant operations support. The company faces converging regulatory exposure across the Nuclear Regulatory Commission (10 CFR 73.54), potential EU Cyber Resilience Act compliance, and NIS2 Directive obligations if products are deployed in European markets. No publicly disclosed security incidents, CVEs, or data breaches have been identified in the last 36 months, consistent with the company's pre-deployment commercial stage.

- **Anchor customers confirmed:** Dow Chemical, Amazon, Energy Northwest, Centrica (11.5 GW backlog)

- **Regulatory alignment:** Pre-application activities with NRC on track; no licensing obstacles identified
- **Manufacturing partnerships:** Toyo Tanso, Doosan Enerbility, IHI securing supply chain capacity

- **Negative margins:** FY2024 gross loss of \$86M on \$84M revenue; burn rate \$237.9M annual free cash flow negative

- **Regulatory uncertainty:** NRC licensing timeline subject to topical review iterations; first operating license not expected until 2031-2032

- **10 CFR 73.54:** On track; cybersecurity program review ongoing
- **EU CRA/NIS2:** Future exposure (2027+ if UK/EU deployments proceed); CRA compliance expected 2027-2028 for Class II critical products
