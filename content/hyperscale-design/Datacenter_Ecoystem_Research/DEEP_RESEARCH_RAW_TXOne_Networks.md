## 4. Technology and Architecture — OT Protocols, Firmware, Network Management, and Security Standards

**TXOne's technology foundation is purpose-built for operational technology environments, prioritizing operational continuity and protocol-native inspection over traditional IT security approaches.** [[68]](https://www.siemens.com/en-us/products/txone-networks-txone-portable-inspector/)[[69]](https://www.txone.com/data-sheets/portable-inspector/)[[70]](https://www.linkedin.com/company/txone-networks-japan/)

**Core DPI Technology: TXODI™ (One-Pass Deep Packet Inspection for Industry)**

TXODI is TXOne's proprietary deep packet inspection engine, **designed to process industrial control system protocols without requiring bidirectional TCP inspection or stateful flow tracking common in IT firewalls**. [[41]](https://builders.intel.com/docs/networkbuilders/txone-networks-secures-semiconductor-manufacturing-ot-with-caswell-network-security-appliances-1698398692.pdf)

### EU NIS2 Directive Applicability

**NIS2 (Directive 2022/2555/EU)** entered full enforcement on October 18, 2024, requiring critical infrastructure operators and essential service providers across the EU to implement cybersecurity measures across Objectives A-D (governance, protection, detection, incident response). [[87]][[90]]

TXOne has published white-paper guidance on NIS2 compliance and positions its products to address these objectives: [[52]](https://media.txone.com/prod/uploads/2023/07/Mastering-the-NIS2-Directive-Achieving-Cybersecurity-Compliance-TXOne-WP-202307.pdf)

|---|---|---|
| **A. Governance** | SenninOne (organizational visibility and policy enforcement); EdgeOne (centralized management) | Board-level risk management; incident response plans; risk assessments |
| **B. Protection** | Edge appliances (segmentation); Stellar (endpoint hardening); Element (vulnerability assessment) | Network segmentation; access control; encryption; virtual patching |
| **C. Detection** | Stellar (EDR); Edge appliances (anomaly detection); SIEM integration | Continuous monitoring; threat intelligence; real-time detection |
| **D. Incident Response** | PSIRT coordination; SenninOne orchestration; logging and evidence preservation | Incident response plans; breach notification (72 hours); third-party coordination |

**Customers in scope:** Organizations in energy (utilities, oil/gas refineries), water supply, digital infrastructure (cloud/CDN), healthcare, food supply, and public administration with >250 employees are in scope for NIS2. Compliance deadline: **October 18, 2024 (past as of research date)**. Critical infrastructure operators with >1,000 employees faced extended deadlines through October 2025. [[87]][[88]]

### EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) — Article 3(1) and Article 7 Classification

The **EU Cyber Resilience Act (2024/1847)** creates new product safety obligations for "products with digital elements" (effectively all software and connected hardware). [[85]][[89]][[91]]

**Article 3(1) Scope:** Products qualify for CRA obligations if they include a digital component that can receive, process, or transmit data and can be directly or indirectly connected to the internet or network. [[85]][[89]]

**TXOne Product Classification (CRA Article 7):** CRA establishes two product classes based on risk:

- **Class I (Lower Risk):** Products with defined cybersecurity requirements and liability frameworks; lighter compliance burden
- **Class II (Higher Risk):** Critical products with stricter SBOM requirements, vulnerability disclosure, update management, and liability provisions

**Current Status:** TXOne has published compliance guidance for customers but **has not publicly classified its specific products as Class I or Class II**. [[85]][[91]] Based on the type of products (network security appliances, endpoint agents), classification as **Class II is likely** given their role in infrastructure security, but this remains unconfirmed. [[56]](https://www.reedsmith.com/our-insights/blogs/viewpoints/102mnj2/eu-cybersecurity-regulatory-update-for-2026-and-beyond/)

**Timeline:** CRA enforcement begins June 2024 for certain provisions; full enforcement phased through 2025-2026. [[56]](https://www.reedsmith.com/our-insights/blogs/viewpoints/102mnj2/eu-cybersecurity-regulatory-update-for-2026-and-beyond/)

## Synthesis: Strategic Implications for B2B Sales

### Key Competitive Positioning

**TXOne has differentiated itself as the "operations-first" OT security vendor** by solving the endemic problem of operational disruption. Unlike IT-centric security tools (EDR, SIEM) adapted for industrial environments, TXOne's architecture prioritizes availability and latency—ensuring that security controls do not slow down time-critical processes. This resonates with manufacturing and utilities customers for whom downtime costs \$125K-\$300K per hour. [[59]](https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20260601da71802/txone-networks-positions-enforceable-protection-as-the-next-evolution-of-ot-cybersecurity)

### Market Traction Indicators

1. **Strong funding trajectory:** \$144.69M in institutional funding over 2022-2024 demonstrates investor conviction and runway for go-to-market expansion. [[8]](https://www.siemens.com/en-us/ecosystem/txone-networks/)[[31]](https://www.windpowerengineering.com/moxa-tackles-security-in-industrial-iot-manufacturing-energy-environments/)
2. **Geographic expansion:** New Americas office (2025) and Japan operations growth signal multi-region market expansion. [[57]](https://www.csoonline.com/article/3568787/eus-nis2-directive-for-cybersecurity-resilience-enters-full-enforcement.html)[[118]]
3. **Product velocity:** Continuous releases (EdgeOne V2.1, SenninOne, Portable Inspector Pro enhancements) indicate mature product-market fit and roadmap confidence. [[16]](https://www.txone.com/blog/sap-cve-2025-exploits-in-the-wild/)[[114]]
4. **Regulatory tailwinds:** NIS2 and EU CRA compliance deadlines create demand pull for products addressing regulatory frameworks. [[84]][[87]][[89]]

### Addressable Market Characteristics

TXOne's customer profile (Carlsberg and others) indicates demand across:

- **Global manufacturers** with multi-country compliance obligations (NIS2, GDPR)
- **Critical infrastructure operators** (utilities, healthcare) with availability SLAs
- **Legacy-heavy environments** unable to tolerate disruptive security updates
- **Regulated supply chains** (automotive, semiconductors) requiring TISAX/industry-specific standards
