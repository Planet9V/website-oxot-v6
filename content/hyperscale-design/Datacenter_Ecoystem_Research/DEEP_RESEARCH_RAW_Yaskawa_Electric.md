## Executive Summary

**Yaskawa Electric Corporation** is a **Japanese industrial automation leader** established in **1915**, headquartered in **Kitakyushu, Fukuoka, Japan**. With **12,833 consolidated employees** across **30 countries** and **12 production sites**, Yaskawa dominates the global motion control and robotics markets. As of June 2026, the company operates with a market capitalization of approximately **¥1.70 trillion** and maintains listing on the Tokyo Prime Market (Ticker: TYO 6506). 

**FY2024 financial results reveal a challenged period**: Revenue declined **6.6% year-over-year to ¥537.7 billion**, while operating profit compressed to **¥50.2 billion (9.3% margin)**—down from **11.5% in FY2023** and **12.3% in FY2022**. Operating margin compression across all segments signals cost pressures despite a resilient robotics division. The company maintains a **strong balance sheet** with equity of **¥431.2 billion** and net debt-to-equity of **0.16**, though capital expenditure programs are accelerating (¥45.0 billion planned for FY2024).

**Product portfolio strength remains unquestioned**: Yaskawa produces more than **60 distinct industrial robot models**, spanning collaborative robots (HC-Series 10-30 kg payloads), heavy-payload arms (MH-series up to 900 kg), and specialized platforms (MOTOMAN NEXT with AI integration). Motion Control revenue (44% of total) derives from **Sigma-7 and Sigma-5 servo motor families**, **GA-series industrial AC drives**, and integrated machine controllers (iC9200, MPiec series). The company achieved **IEC 62443-4-1 certification** (December 2022) for secure product development—a critical standard for industrial control systems.

**Critical research gaps**: No publicly disclosed CVEs, security incidents, or PSIRT advisories appear in authoritative databases for the past 36 months. The company has not publicly stated its EU Cyber Resilience Act product classification status, NIS2 Directive applicability, or possession of ISO 27001/SOC 2 certifications. ## Conclusion

Yaskawa Electric Corporation remains a **credible global automation leader** with proven market presence and financial stability. The company's organizational structure is transparent, management team experienced, and product portfolio comprehensive. However, **material information gaps** exist in regulatory compliance status (EU CRA, NIS2), cybersecurity certification portfolio (beyond IEC 62443-4-1), and security incident transparency. 

For B2B sales professionals, **Yaskawa represents a viable primary supplier**, particularly for motion control, robotics, and system integration applications. However, due diligence should include direct requests for security certifications, regulatory compliance roadmaps, and PSIRT procedures—standard enterprise procurement practices for industrial automation systems.

The recent leadership transition (May 2026) and margin compression pressures warrant ongoing strategic monitoring, but do not fundamentally alter Yaskawa's competitive positioning or financial stability. Customers with strong negotiating leverage may benefit from current pricing discussions, given revenue headwinds and competitive intensity in the robotics market.
