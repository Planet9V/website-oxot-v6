## Executive Summary

FuelCell Energy, Inc. (NASDAQ: FCEL) is a publicly traded clean energy technology manufacturer headquartered in Danbury, Connecticut, specializing in molten carbonate fuel cell (MCFC) systems for distributed power generation, microgrids, and emerging hyperscale data center applications. The company reported **\$158.2 million in FY2025 revenue (+41.1% YoY)** with a **\$1.19 billion backlog**, but remains unprofitable with a **\$(191.4) million net loss** and significant operating cash burn. Strategic pivot to AI/hyperscaler data center market (2025-2026) is reshaping the company's target customer base away from utility-scale wastewater treatment toward 100+ MW data center power deployments. Severe geographic revenue concentration in South Korea (46% from single customer Gyeonggi Green Energy) represents near-term liquidity strength but long-term dependency risk. Critical gaps exist in published cybersecurity governance, product security incident response, and regulatory compliance documentation—presenting both risk and opportunity for security-focused channel partners and system integrators seeking entry into emerging grid-connected distributed power market.

Despite 41% FY2025 revenue growth and \$1.19B backlog, company remains **deeply unprofitable** with **\$(191.4)M net loss** and **\$(125.3)M negative operating cash flow**. This necessitates:

1. **Continued Equity Dilution:** \$92.6M equity raise FY2025; likely continued dilution to fund operations
