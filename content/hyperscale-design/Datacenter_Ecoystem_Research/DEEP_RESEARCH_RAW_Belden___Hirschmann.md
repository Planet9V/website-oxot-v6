## 5. REGULATORY EXPOSURE: COMPLIANCE LANDSCAPE

### EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) — December 10, 2024 Entry into Force

**Scope & Applicability:**
The Cyber Resilience Act applies to "products with digital elements" — a category that **encompasses the vast majority of Belden's product portfolio** including industrial switches, firewalls, IoT routers, and software platforms [[93]][[98]].

**Article 3(1) Scope Determination:**
Belden hardware products with embedded firmware or software components (Hirschmann switches, EAGLE firewalls, NetModule routers, macmon software) qualify as "products with digital elements" triggering CRA obligations [[93]][[98]].

**Article 7 Classification Risk:**
The CRA distinguishes between Class I and Class II products based on cybersecurity risk profile:
- **Class I products:** Basic cybersecurity baseline (self-assessed)
- **Class II products:** Higher-risk products requiring third-party conformity assessment bodies (notified bodies)

**Preliminary Assessment:** Hirschmann firewalls (EAGLE series) with intrusion prevention and threat detection functions likely fall under **Class II classification**, requiring notified body involvement [[93]][[98]].

**Compliance Timeline:**
| Milestone | Deadline | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Notification of conformity assessment bodies | June 11, 2026 | EC Commission notification of qualified assessment bodies |
| Active vulnerability & incident reporting | September 11, 2026 | Vulnerability disclosure and incident reporting requirements begin |

## CONCLUSIONS

Belden Inc. represents a mature, financially stable OT/industrial networking company undergoing strategic transformation toward IT/OT convergence through the \$1.85 billion Ruckus Networks acquisition. The company's core industrial products hold robust IEC 62443-4-2 certifications, command strong positions in power utility and industrial automation markets, and generate solid cash flows.

However, material regulatory compliance risks exist, particularly the **unresolved EU Cyber Resilience Act SBOM requirement** (critical gap with Dec 11, 2027 deadline) and **17+ unpatched or recently-disclosed CVEs** in core firewall platforms. The absence of published SOC 2 and ISO 27001 certifications raises questions about enterprise security governance maturity.

**For B2B sales and procurement purposes:** Belden is an appropriate supplier for industrial OT applications with solid market position and NERC CIP credentials. For enterprise IT/cloud infrastructure use cases, the post-Ruckus integration (closing H2 2026) will significantly expand applicability. **Mandatory due diligence on CRA/SBOM compliance and CVE patch status is required before enterprise deployments.**
