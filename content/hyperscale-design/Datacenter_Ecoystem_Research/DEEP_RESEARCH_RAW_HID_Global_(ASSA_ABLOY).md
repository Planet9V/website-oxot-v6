### CVE Details & Patching

#### **CVE-2024-23806** (Sensitive Data Extraction)

- **Severity**: CVSS 5.3 (moderate, physical attack vector)
- **Affected Products**: 
 - iCLASS SE reader configuration cards (all firmware versions up to 8.6.0.4)
 - OMNIKEY 5x27 series readers used for configuration/encoding
- **Root Cause**: **Design flaw** — reader configuration cards store **encryption keys and administrator credentials in plaintext or poorly encrypted form**; physical access to card (reader disassembly) enables key extraction
- **Exploitation**: Requires physical access to reader hardware; not remotely exploitable; used for **lateral movement** in compromised facilities
- **Mitigation**: 
 - **Firmware patch**: 8.6.0.4+ (released ~Q1 2024)
 - **Hardware option**: Shield Card (protective cover preventing physical access without visible tampering)
 - **Procedure**: Rotate reader encryption keys after patch deployment
- **CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) Status**: Not yet confirmed in CISA KEV catalog as of June 2026 (relatively low exploitation likelihood due to physical requirements) [[51]](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cyber-resilience-act)[[59]](https://www.tuvsud.com/en-us/industries/manufacturing/machinery-and-robotics/iec-62443-industrial-security)[[63]](https://www.rsa.com/resources/blog/zero-trust/preparing-for-the-nis2-directive-safeguarding-critical-infrastructure-against-cyberthreats/)[[65]](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/nis2-directive)

#### **CVE-2024-22388** (Secure Channel Downgrade)

- **Severity**: CVSS 5.9 (moderate, local attack vector)
- **Affected Products**: 
 - iCLASS SE Reader CP1000 (encoder/configurator)
 - OMNIKEY 5x27 series readers when used as encoder
- **Root Cause**: **Protocol implementation flaw** — Secure Channel Protocol (SCP) implementation permits downgrade from encrypted to unencrypted mode under certain conditions; attackers (with local access to reader configuration interface) can force downgrade
- **Exploitation**: Requires local network access or physical proximity to reader; enables **credential key sniffing** during configuration
- **Mitigation**: 
 - **Firmware patch**: 8.6.0.4+ (released Q1 2024)
 - **Monitoring**: Enable reader audit logging to detect protocol downgrade attempts
- **Patch Status**: Widely deployed; HID recommends **urgent firmware upgrade for all CP1000 and 5x27 readers** [[60]](https://blog.gradum.io/compare/nis2-vs-nerc-cip)[[61]](https://atlas-advisory.eu/en/insights/nis2-directive-compliance-guide)
