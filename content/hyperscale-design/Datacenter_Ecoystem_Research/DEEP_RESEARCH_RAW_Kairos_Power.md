## 4. TECHNOLOGY AND ARCHITECTURE

**Core Reactor Technology: Molten Salt-Cooled Pebble Bed Design**

Kairos Power's KP-FHR represents a significant departure from conventional light-water reactor (LWR) architecture, combining proven molten salt coolant principles with TRISO fuel innovation:

**Coolant System Architecture:**
- **Coolant Type:** FLiBe (2LiF-BeF₂)—a chemically stable, non-corrosive fluoride salt mixture
- **Operating Temperature:** 650°C outlet (vs. 320°C for conventional LWRs)
- **Operating Pressure:** Near-atmospheric (0.1-0.5 MPa vs. 15+ MPa for LWRs), reducing vessel wall thickness and cost
- **Thermal Properties:** High volumetric heat capacity enables efficient heat transfer with lower flow rates
- **Safety Advantage:** No phase change (boiling) possible at operating conditions; passive natural convection cooling on loss of forced circulation [[17]](https://www.kairospower.com/updates/kairos-power-breaks-ground-on-salt-production-facility-to-make-molten-salt-coolant-for-advanced-reactors)

**Fuel Architecture:**
- **Fuel Form:** TRISO pebbles (spherical, ~6 cm diameter)
- **Particle Coating:** Tri-layer ceramic coating (PyC/SiC/PyC) provides fission product retention
- **Matrix Material:** Graphite binder embedded with ~10,000 TRISO particles per pebble
- **Enrichment:** 19.75% uranium-235 (low-enriched uranium, LEU) with future HALEU capability
- **Burnup Design:** Target 16+ GWd/MTIHM (gigawatt-days per metric ton of initial heavy metal)

**Reactor Core Configuration:**
- **Core Type:** Pebble bed design with online refueling capability
- **Refueling Method:** Fresh pebbles continuously fed into top of core; burned-out pebbles removed from bottom during operation (no shutdown required)
- **Core Internals:** Graphite structure material (Ibiden ET-10 grade, rated 650°C)
- **Vessel Material:** 316H stainless steel for primary system components

**Primary Coolant Loop Design:**
- **Single-phase coolant circulation:** Forced convection during normal operation via electromagnetic pumps (no moving parts in traditional sense)
- **Passive cooling pathway:** Natural convection circulation in air-cooled heat exchanger during loss-of-forced-circulation scenarios
- **Heat transport:** Direct heating of air via recuperative heat exchanger, eliminating secondary loops

**Balance of Plant (BOP):**
- **Power Conversion:** Brayton cycle direct air cooling (simplified vs. Rankine steam cycle in conventional reactors)
- **Auxiliary Systems:** Simplified design due to lower pressure, near-ambient operating conditions
- **Modular Construction:** Prefabricated modules transported and assembled on-site
