## PAGE 3 OF 6: OT SECURITY ANALYSIS & REGULATORY EXPOSURE

### 3.1 Cybersecurity Certification Status

| Product Line / Division | IEC 62443-4-1 | IEC 62443-4-2 | ISO 27001 | IEC 62443-3-3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Grid Software Division** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Mark VIe Control System**| Yes | No | No | Yes |
| **Multilin Relay Systems** | Yes | No | No | No |

**Security Program Profile:** GE Vernova maintains IEC 62443-4-1 (secure product development) lifecycle certification for its Grid Software division. Selected grid automation components hold IEC 62443-3-3 certifications. However, the Mark VIe DCS and Multilin network relay lines lack formal IEC 62443-4-2 component level certification.

### 3.2 EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) Exposure
All GE Vernova grid controllers and software qualify as products with digital elements:
*   **Mark VIe & Multilin Relays:** Class II (Important) products due to their operational roles in critical energy infrastructure, requiring third-party security audits.
*   **ADMS Grid Software:** Class II product subject to strict vulnerability management and reporting.

**Penalty Exposure:** Non-compliance with CRA rules exposes GE Vernova to fines up to 2.5% of global annual turnover, representing a maximum potential fine of approximately $920 million based on FY2024 revenue.

### 3.3 Infrastructure Regulations (NERC CIP & NRC 10 CFR 73.54)
*   **NERC CIP:** Applies to all GE Vernova control systems and software sold to North American electric utilities. Compliance focus is on physical safety, system access controls (CIP-007), configuration management (CIP-010), and supply chain risk management (CIP-013).
*   **NRC 10 CFR 73.54:** Nuclear turbines and steam control systems (such as those supplied to civilian nuclear operators) must pass NRC Design Certification audits to verify secure software development.

### 3.4 Publicly Disclosed Vulnerabilities (Last 24 Months)
GE Vernova operates an active PSIRT program and coordinates disclosures with CISA:
*   **CVE-2025-27257 (Multilin N60):** Firmware signature validation bypass vulnerability permitting arbitrary code execution (CVSS 9.8). The server accepted unsigned firmware updates due to client-side signature check defects. Patch released in early 2025.
