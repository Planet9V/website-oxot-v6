### EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) Applicability (Unresolved)

The EU **Cyber Resilience Act (2024)** requires assessment of products with digital elements under **Article 3(1)** and classification as **Class I (standard) or Class II (critical)** under **Article 7** [[34]](https://www.european-cyber-resilience-act.com/Cyber_Resilience_Act_Article_3.html)[[105]][[109]].

**Axiomtek Product Applicability Assessment:**

Many Axiomtek products contain digital elements with network connectivity (Ethernet, MQTT, HTTP/REST, SNMP, Modbus support) and would **reasonably fall within CRA Article 3(1) scope** as \"products with digital elements intended for consumers or businesses.\" However, **the CRA requires manufacturer self-assessment** rather than third-party determination. 

**No Axiomtek-specific CRA compliance documentation was found.** Axiomtek has not published public statements clarifying which product lines meet Article 3(1) scope or how products are classified under Article 7. This is a **material compliance question for European buyers** and should be addressed directly with Axiomtek legal/compliance teams during procurement.

**NERC CIP (North American Electric Reliability Corporation Critical Infrastructure Protection):** Applies to **bulk electric system operators and generators** in North America. Axiomtek products used in utility SCADA or generation systems may fall in scope. **No NERC CIP certification or compliance statement found** for Axiomtek products.

**NRC 10 CFR 73.54 (Cybersecurity Regulations for Nuclear Power Plants):** Applies to nuclear facility control systems. **Axiomtek does not appear to target the nuclear sector** based on product portfolio and certifications; no NRC compliance documentation found.
