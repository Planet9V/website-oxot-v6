## 5. Regulatory Exposure — EU CRA, NIS2, NERC CIP, NRC, GDPR, and Cybersecurity Incidents

**EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation EU 2024/2847) — Scope and DeltaV Status:**

The Cyber Resilience Act, effective December 11, 2027, establishes mandatory cybersecurity requirements for "products with digital elements" sold into the EU market. [[69]](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/EN/legal-content/summary/horizontal-cybersecurity-requirements-for-products-with-digital-elements.html) [[70]](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ:L_202402847)

**Scope Assessment for DeltaV:**
DeltaV qualifies as a "product with digital elements" under Article 3(1) because it:
- Contains embedded computing (controllers, workstations, network cards)
- Performs data processing and communication via network protocols (Ethernet-APL, PROFINET, EtherNet/IP)
- Connects to networks or the Internet (via DeltaV Edge Environment, REST API, OPC UA interfaces) [[70]](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ:L_202402847)

**Article 7 Classification (Class I vs. Class II) — DETERMINATION UNCERTAIN:**
The CRA requires manufacturers to classify products as either "Class I" (standard requirements) or "Class II" (important products) if the product "performs a function carrying significant risk to critical infrastructure or society, including network management, configuration control, or processing of personal data." [[70]](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ:L_202402847)

DeltaV likely qualifies as **Class II ("Important Product")** based on:
- Function in critical infrastructure (energy, water, pharmaceuticals, chemical processing)
- Network management capabilities (DeltaV Edge Environment, PROFINET/EtherNet/IP coordination)
- Central system functions (configuration control across distributed assets)

**However, no public Emerson statement confirms Class II designation.** This likely awaits formal EU regulatory guidance or company-initiated classification disclosure.
