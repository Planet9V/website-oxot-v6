### 3.3 CRA-by-Design Opportunity (NEW PRODUCTS)

nVent's November 2025 announcement of new modular data center liquid cooling solutions is a **specific, time-bound Tetrel opportunity:**

> Under **Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2392** (governing Annex III classifications), data center liquid cooling controllers and smart CDUs fall under **Class I** (as digital industrial control and network management interfaces). If sold in the EU after the December 11, 2027 deadline (with ENISA vulnerability reporting active as of September 11, 2026), they must comply. These products are being designed NOW. nVent needs IEC 62443 security requirements built into the product design specifications — this is exactly what Tetrel's IEC 62443 Gap Assessment (early stage) and SBOM Program initiation services deliver.

**This is the strongest possible entry: new product line, regulatory requirement, clear delivery date.**
