- Request formal CRA Article 3(1) compliance assessment and implementation roadmap
- Require published supply chain security attestation
- Demand incident response SLA and CISO engagement in procurement
- Negotiate contractual incident notification obligations (within 72 hours)

**For Financial Services (Payment/PSD2)**:
- Leverage PSD2 compliance and PCI-DSS support as table-stakes capability
- Position nShield HSM as **stronger security posture than cloud-only alternatives**
- Offer managed HSM services (nSaaS) for cost-conscious acquirers

**For Enterprise IT (Large Corporations)**:
- Emphasize **15+ years of nShield market maturity** and low CVE frequency
- Bundle with Onfido for **identity-centric security strategy** (zero-trust, passwordless authentication)
- Leverage reference customers (Con-way, Cadence Design Systems, Delta Dental)

**For Hyperscalers (AWS, Azure, GCP)**:
- Position nShield as **hybrid-cloud HSM** complementing managed HSM services
- Emphasize multi-tenancy isolation and regulatory compliance (FEDRAMP, etc.)

## Conclusion

Entrust Corporation is a **mature, well-established cryptographic security and digital identity solutions provider** with **strong product certification pedigree** (FIPS 140-3, Common Criteria EAL4+, eIDAS QSCD) and **global market reach** (150+ countries, 4,000+ partners, \$916.7M FY2024 revenue). 

The **nShield HSM product family** represents **tier-1 position in enterprise cryptographic key management**, competing directly with Thales/Luna and SafeNet offerings. The **Onfido acquisition** signals Entrust's strategic pivot toward **identity-centric security**, differentiating from pure-play HSM vendors.

However, **recent security incidents** (Nov 2025 ransomware, 2024 CA misissuance, June 2022 breach) underscore that **strong product security does not guarantee operational security excellence**. B2B buyers should conduct **independent security due diligence** focusing on incident response procedures, regulatory compliance roadmaps, and supply chain transparency before making large-scale procurement commitments.

**Data gaps remain** in financial metrics (3-year history, growth rates, debt levels), customer concentration, and detailed regulatory applicability (EU CRA, NIS2, GDPR). These gaps reflect Entrust's private company status and limited public disclosure practices; customers requiring comprehensive vendor intelligence should engage Entrust directly for financial attestations, reference customers, and regulatory compliance documentation.
