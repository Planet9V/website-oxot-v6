## 4. Technology and Architecture — OT-Native Design, Protocols, and Cybersecurity Certifications

### OT/IoT Protocol Support and Deep Packet Inspection

The Dragos Platform's core technical differentiator is its support for **600+ industrial control system and operational technology protocols** through Layer-7 (application layer) deep packet inspection (DPI). This distinguishes it from traditional IT-focused security tools, which typically support only IT networking protocols (TCP/IP, HTTP, DNS, etc.) and cannot inspect or understand the semantics of OT communication.

**Protocol Categories Supported:**
- **Legacy Industrial:** MODBUS (serial and TCP variants), PROFIBUS, PROFINET, DNP3, EtherCAT
- **Vendor-Specific:** Siemens S7 (STEP 7), Rockwell Allen-Bradley DF1 and PCCC, GE SRTP, Mitsubishi MELSEC
- **Open Standards:** OPC UA, MQTT, CoAP, REST APIs
- **Specialized:** BACnet (building automation), Modbus/TCP, EtherNet/IP, GOOSE (power systems)
- **Emerging IoT:** HTTP, MQTT, CoAP variants used in connected industrial devices [[22]](https://www.dragos.com/blog/isa-iec-62443-standards)[[24]](https://www.dragos.com/blog/isa-iec-62443-concepts)[[27]](https://www.dragos.com/insights/nerc-cip-compliance)

This protocol diversity enables asset discovery and threat detection across the full spectrum of OT device types—from legacy 1980s–1990s SCADA systems to modern connected IoT devices, industrial routers, and programmable logic controllers (PLCs).

### Data Collection Architecture

The Dragos Platform employs a non-disruptive, passive-first architecture to minimize risk to operational systems:

**Primary Collection Method:** Passive network monitoring via mirror ports (SPAN ports) or network TAPs, capturing traffic without inserting devices inline. This "listen-only" approach eliminates risk of network disruption or latency impact. [[14]](https://dragos.brightspotcdn.com/e0/eb/64cfef5f423f8ce7e0ff512e3bc3/dragos-platform-datasheet-09-25.pdf)

**Active Collection:** For situations where passive monitoring is insufficient (air-gapped networks, isolated sensors), Dragos Agent performs active protocol queries on Windows endpoints within the OT network. Queries are designed to be safe and non-disruptive to industrial operations. [[14]](https://dragos.brightspotcdn.com/e0/eb/64cfef5f423f8ce7e0ff512e3bc3/dragos-platform-datasheet-09-25.pdf)

**Edge Processing:** Dragos employs distributed analytics with data reduction at the edge—raw packet data is analyzed locally on sensors to extract metadata, flows, and events before transmission to cloud or on-premises aggregation points. This reduces bandwidth requirements for geographically distributed sites. [[22]](https://www.dragos.com/blog/isa-iec-62443-standards)[[24]](https://www.dragos.com/blog/isa-iec-62443-concepts)
