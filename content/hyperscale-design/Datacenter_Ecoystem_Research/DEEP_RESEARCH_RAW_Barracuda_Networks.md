### Lessons from CVE-2023-2868 for Procurement

The CVE-2023-2868 incident established several critical procurement considerations:

1. **Hardware Replacement Risk**: Security appliances can experience firmware-level compromise requiring full hardware replacement (not just software patching)
2. **Extended Zero-Day Windows**: Critical appliances may be exploited for months before discovery
3. **Threat Actor Targeting**: Nation-state actors actively target security infrastructure
4. **Patch Sufficiency Questions**: Even when vendors issue patches, customers may need to assess whether patching alone is sufficient or hardware replacement is warranted
