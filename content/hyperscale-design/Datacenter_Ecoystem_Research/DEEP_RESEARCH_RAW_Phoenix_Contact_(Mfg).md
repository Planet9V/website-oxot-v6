### 2025 Vulnerabilities (Escalating Frequency)

**Most Critical: QUINT4-UPS EtherNet/IP Vulnerabilities** (October 2025):
- **Products Affected**: QUINT4-UPS Series uninterruptible power supplies with EtherNet/IP connectivity
- **CVE IDs**: CVE-2025-41703, CVE-2025-41704, CVE-2025-41705, CVE-2025-41706, CVE-2025-41707 (5 distinct vulnerabilities)
- **Severity Breakdown**:
  - **CVE-2025-41703** (Modbus Protocol Abuse): CVSS 7.5 (High) - **Unauthenticated Modbus communication allows remote attackers to manipulate UPS outputs** (charge/discharge, bypass, critical operations) without authentication; **remains unfixed by design in Version Control** because devices are architecturally designed for closed-network deployment; vendor remediation = network segmentation requirement, not firmware patch
  - CVE-2025-41704, 41705, 41706, 41707: CVSS 5.x-7.x range (Medium-High) - Information disclosure, configuration modification
- **Attack Scenario**: Attacker on same network segment can override UPS operation logic via raw Modbus/TCP commands, triggering unintended charge cycles, emergency shutdowns, or power delivery disruptions
- **Remediation Strategy**: No firmware fix available; requires network isolation, firewall rules, or industrial demilitarized zone (IDMZ) deployment
- **Compliance Impact**: Critical for NIS2/CRA compliance assessments; UPS systems are typically classified as critical infrastructure equipment
- **Source**: [[6]](https://cyberdanube.com/security-research/multiple-vulnerabilities-in-phoenix-contact-usv-quint4-ups/) [[8]](https://seclists.org/fulldisclosure/2025/Oct/12) [[11]](https://www.securityweek.com/vulnerabilities-allow-disruption-of-phoenix-contact-ups-devices/)
