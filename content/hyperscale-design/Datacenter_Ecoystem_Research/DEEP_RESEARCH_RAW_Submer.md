## 4. TECHNOLOGY & ARCHITECTURE — Technical Implementation & Certifications

### Core Technology Foundation

**Thermal Management Architecture**: [[12]](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/submer) [[16]](https://submer.com/immersion-cooling/)
- **Core Principle**: Single-phase liquid immersion cooling—submerging entire server components (CPUs, GPUs, memory, storage) in non-conductive dielectric fluid
- **Scope**: Chip-to-cluster level thermal management (not just CPU-level)
- **Heat Dissipation**: Eliminates reliance on air-cooled fans and high-velocity air circulation; heat transferred directly through liquid to centralized cooling plant

**Computational Validation**: [[14]](https://www.simscale.com/customers/submer-technologies/)
- **Tool**: SimScale cloud-based CFD (computational fluid dynamics) simulation platform
- **Purpose**: Validates thermal performance, flow uniformity, and edge-case heat dissipation before physical deployment
- **Outcome**: Enables confidence in new thermal designs without extensive physical prototyping

Submer Technologies S.L. has evolved from a specialized cooling vendor (2015) into a **full-stack AI infrastructure provider** commanding \$500M valuation and serving hyperscalers, telecom operators, and government entities globally. **For B2B sales research and procurement**, Submer represents a **high-growth, technology-differentiated platform** well-positioned for immersion-cooled AI infrastructure, but requiring **direct security and compliance due diligence** before large enterprise, government, or regulated utility deployments. The February 2026 Radian Arc acquisition and Q1 2026 InferX launch represent **major strategic inflection points**, suggesting the company is transitioning toward integrated cloud infrastructure services with 2027–2028 profitability inflection.
