### 3.1 Natrium® Reactor — Advanced Nuclear Power System

**Product Description:** The **Natrium® reactor** is TerraPower's flagship commercial product — a **sodium-cooled fast reactor (SFR)** with **integrated molten salt thermal energy storage** [[35]](https://www.terrapower.com/natrium/) [[37]](https://www.terrapower.com/NRC-Approves-Natrium-Reactor-Construction-Permit).

**Key Specifications:**
- **Electrical Capacity:** 345 MW nameplate (continuous baseload operation)
- **Peak Output (with Storage):** 500 MW electrical peak capacity over 5+ hours of thermal storage discharge [[35]](https://www.terrapower.com/natrium/)
- **Thermal Power:** 840 MWth per unit [[33]](https://www.terrapower.com/downloads/Process-Heat.pdf)
- **Operating Temperature Profile:**
  - Primary loop hot leg: 540°C (1,004°F) [[33]](https://www.terrapower.com/downloads/Process-Heat.pdf)
  - Atmospheric pressure operation (unlike pressurized water reactors), with sodium boiling point >1,616°F providing substantial safety margin [[33]](https://www.terrapower.com/downloads/Process-Heat.pdf)
  - Passive cooling capability without external power during blackout conditions [[33]](https://www.terrapower.com/downloads/Process-Heat.pdf)

**Thermal Energy Storage System:**
- **Technology:** Molten salt-based thermal buffer system [[33]](https://www.terrapower.com/downloads/Process-Heat.pdf) [[35]](https://www.terrapower.com/natrium/)
- **Capability:** Allows reactor to maintain 100% thermal power while modulating electrical output from 30% to 150% of nominal rating [[33]](https://www.terrapower.com/downloads/Process-Heat.pdf)
- **Grid Flexibility:** 10% ramp-rate capability per minute (versus 2.5–5% for conventional light water reactors), enabling demand response and data center power scheduling [[33]](https://www.terrapower.com/downloads/Process-Heat.pdf)
- **Duration:** >5.5 hours of thermal storage [[35]](https://www.terrapower.com/natrium/)

- **Fuel Type:** HALEU (High-Assay Low-Enriched Uranium) metallic fuel in HALEU-based form [[33]](https://www.terrapower.com/downloads/Process-Heat.pdf) [[39]](https://www.terrapower.com/faq/)

## 4. Technology and Architecture — Nuclear-Specific Specifications

**Critical Finding:** The original request specified technology requirements (OT/IoT firmware platforms, SNMP, Modbus, BACnet, REST protocols, network management cards, SBOM status) that are **not applicable** to TerraPower's product portfolio. **Natrium Reactor Technology Overview:**

**Reactor Type and Design Philosophy:**
- **Classification:** Sodium-cooled fast reactor (SFR), pool-type architecture [[33]](https://www.terrapower.com/downloads/Process-Heat.pdf) [[39]](https://www.terrapower.com/faq/)
- **Neutron Spectrum:** Fast neutron spectrum (versus thermal spectrum in light water reactors), enabling breeding and transmutation capability [[39]](https://www.terrapower.com/faq/)
- **Development Lineage:** Over 70 years of international sodium fast reactor research and operating experience (leveraging EBR-II, FFTF, and international SFR programs) through GE Hitachi partnership [[16]](https://www.terrapower.com/terrapower-and-hd-hyundai-announce-strategic-collaboration-to-scale-global-supply-chain-for-natrium) [[18]](https://www.kedglobal.com/energy/newsView/ked202503120009)

**Operating Conditions and Thermal Performance:**
- **Primary Loop Temperature (Hot Leg):** 540°C / 1,004°F [[33]](https://www.terrapower.com/downloads/Process-Heat.pdf)
- **Safe Operating Margin:** Sodium's boiling point (1,616°F) is substantially above operating temperature, providing intrinsic boiling margin [[33]](https://www.terrapower.com/downloads/Process-Heat.pdf)
- **Pressure Profile:** Atmospheric pressure operation (not pressurized); eliminates pressurized-boundary failure modes inherent to light water reactors [[33]](https://www.terrapower.com/downloads/Process-Heat.pdf)
- **Passive Cooling:** Reactor design enables decay heat removal without external cooling water supply or active pump operation during loss-of-power scenarios [[33]](https://www.terrapower.com/downloads/Process-Heat.pdf)

**Thermal Energy Storage Integration:**
- **Medium:** Molten salt thermal storage (composition not detailed in sources)
- **Thermal Capacity:** Enables reactor core to operate continuously at 100% thermal power while electrical output modulates from 30% to 150% of nominal [[33]](https://www.terrapower.com/downloads/Process-Heat.pdf)
- **Grid Services:** 10% electrical ramp capability per minute, versus 2.5–5% for conventional reactors, providing valuable grid ancillary services and demand-following capability [[33]](https://www.terrapower.com/downloads/Process-Heat.pdf)

**Fuel System and Supply Chain:**
- **HALEU Metallization:** Uranium metal form in High-Assay Low-Enriched Uranium (HALEU) composition, achieving 150 MWd/kg specific energy versus 68–70 MWd/kg for conventional LWR uranium oxide fuel [[33]](https://www.terrapower.com/downloads/Process-Heat.pdf)
- **Fuel Facility:** Global Nuclear Fuel-Americas (GNF-A) awarded contract to design and construct Natrium Fuel Facility in Wilmington, North Carolina (>$200 million investment); expected to produce HALEU fuel assemblies at scale [[20]](https://www.hitachi-hgne.co.jp/en/news/2022/2022news04.html) [[22]](https://www.gevernova.com/news/press-releases/global-nuclear-fuel-and-terrapower-announce-natrium-fuel-facility)

## 5. Regulatory Exposure and Compliance Framework

**Applicable Regulatory Regimes:**

**NRC 10 CFR 73.54 — Nuclear Cybersecurity (DIRECTLY APPLICABLE):**
TerraPower's Natrium reactor is subject to U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission cybersecurity requirements under 10 CFR 73.54, which mandates protection of digital computer and communication systems at nuclear power facilities [[27]](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML2406/ML24061A055.pdf) [[28]](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML0903/ML090340159.pdf) [[29]](https://www.industrialdefender.com/blog/guide-to-nei-08-09-compliance). Specific compliance mechanisms include:
- NEI 08-09 Cyber Security Plan for Nuclear Power Reactors (Revision 7) framework [[27]](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML2406/ML24061A055.pdf)
- Regulatory Guide 5.71 "Cyber Security Programs for Nuclear Facilities" (Revision 1, issued February 13, 2023) [[32]](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2023/02/13/2023-02941/cyber-security-programs-for-nuclear-power-reactors)
- Defense-in-depth cybersecurity posture with administrative, physical, and technical controls [[28]](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML0903/ML090340159.pdf)
- Periodic security reviews and testing [[28]](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML0903/ML090340159.pdf)

**NERC CIP Standards (NOT DIRECTLY APPLICABLE):** While NERC CIP applies to bulk power system operators and certain critical infrastructure, TerraPower as a reactor developer/operator is not a bulk power system entity and thus not subject to NERC CIP requirements [[30]](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/RL34331).

**EU Cyber Resilience Act Article 3(1) (CONDITIONALLY APPLICABLE):**
Should TerraPower export Natrium reactor technology, components, or hardware to European Union markets, the **EU Cyber Resilience Act (2024/2847)** Article 3(1) may classify Natrium systems as "products with digital elements." However, nuclear reactor products are subject to separate EU nuclear regulatory frameworks (Euratom Directive 2014/87/Euratom) and may have specific exemptions. EU regulatory applicability would require legal analysis beyond available sources. **No current EU operational deployments of Natrium have been publicly announced.** [[14]](https://www.terrapower.com/news)

**NIS2 Directive (POTENTIALLY APPLICABLE):**
The **EU NIS2 Directive (Directive (EU) 2022/2555)** would apply to TerraPower operations in EU member states if the company operates critical infrastructure or offers critical infrastructure services. Given the company's nascent operational status in the EU (UK regulatory engagement commenced October 28, 2025, with acceptance February 19, 2026), full NIS2 applicability remains uncertain pending UK regulatory determinations post-Brexit. [[14]](https://www.terrapower.com/news)

TerraPower processes employee, customer, and research data. To the extent the company operates in EU jurisdictions or processes EU resident data, GDPR compliance is mandatory. Specific GDPR policies and data handling procedures have not been disclosed in public sources.
