3. **Offer "scaling incentives"** for multi-CDU deployments (1-3 units: standard pricing; 4-10 units: 15% discount; 10+: 25% discount)
4. **Bundle service/support** into tiered pricing (basic/standard/premium service levels)

### Key Sales Enablement Gaps Requiring Field Leadership Support

1. **Cybersecurity positioning:** Field team needs documented security roadmap, PSIRT contact, and CRA/NIS2 compliance status before enterprise sales
2. **Financial validation:** Provide Ecolab analyst reports and investor materials to support forward-looking value proposition
3. **Regulatory compliance:** Create EU-specific collateral addressing CRA Article 3(1) scope and Article 7 classification
4. **Technical security review:** Establish process for providing detailed Redfish API, SNMP, Modbus security documentation to security-conscious customers
