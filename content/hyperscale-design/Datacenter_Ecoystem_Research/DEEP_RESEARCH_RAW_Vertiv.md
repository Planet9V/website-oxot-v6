## Executive Summary

**Vertiv Holdings Co is the world's #1 vendor in thermal management and #1 in large 3-phase UPS (>250 kVA), serving every major hyperscaler and a global installed base of networked power, cooling, and IT management devices** that represent both a significant OT attack surface and a compelling entry point for OT cybersecurity offerings [[139]](https://www.vertiv.com/49c127/globalassets/documents/infographics/vertiv-ig-en-gl-fact_sheet_final_103563_06.pdf). With **\$10.2 billion in FY2025 revenue (+27.7% YoY)**, a **\$15.0 billion order backlog (up 109% YoY)**, and a market capitalization of approximately **\$115.4 billion** as of June 2026 [[58]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1674101/000167410126000008/0001674101-26-000008.txt) [[195]](https://s205.q4cdn.com/554782763/files/doc_financials/2025/q4/Vertiv_Fourth-Quarter-2025-Results-Presentation.pdf) [[96]](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vrt/), Vertiv is experiencing a structural demand inflection driven by AI infrastructure.

From a cybersecurity sales perspective, four signals make Vertiv a priority target account:

- **Two critical CVEs (CVSS v3.1 9.8) disclosed May 2025** affecting the RDU101 and IS-UNITY NMC families — authentication bypass (CWE-288) and stack-based buffer overflow (CWE-121) — reported by Claroty Team82, patched via firmware updates [[83]](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-140-10).
- **No public SBOM, no IEC 62443-4-1 SDL certification**, and only one product line (RDU120) with IEC 62443-4-2 SL2 certification — significant gaps relative to EU Cyber Resilience Act obligations taking effect December 2027 [[11]](https://www.vertiv.com/en-emea/about/news-and-insights/articles/blog-posts/from-compliance-to-confidence-rethinking-cybersecurity-strategy-for-modern-infrastructure/) [[63]](https://www.vertiv.com/en-us/support/software-download/monitoring/liebert-intellislot-communications-interface-cards/).
- **Active executive churn** — new CFO (November 2025), new CTO (January 2026), new CIO (June 2025), new CPO (May 2026), and a vacant CMO role — creates sales entry points through relationships with incoming leaders [[3]](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/vertiv-names-craig-chamberlin-as-chief-financial-officer-302581923.html) [[1]](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/vertiv-announces-cto-leadership-changes-302575867.html) [[5]](https://www.vertiv.com/en-asia/about/news-and-events/news-releases/vertiv-names-mike-giresi-as-global-cio/) [[70]](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/vertiv-appoints-frieda-he-as-chief-procurement-officer-302762565.html).
- **A four-pillar product security organization** reporting to CIO Mike Giresi, with SecurityScorecard already embedded as the TPRM vendor [[10]](https://securityscorecard.com/resources/case-studies/vertiv/) — indicating a security-aware buyer with established procurement processes for cybersecurity tools.

### NIS2 Directive

Vertiv is **not an NIS2 essential entity** (it is a manufacturer/supplier, not an operator of critical infrastructure). However, **supply chain exposure is material**: Vertiv's customers — hyperscale data centers, colocation providers, healthcare, utilities, telecoms — are NIS2-covered essential entities. Under Article 21, these customers must assess and impose cybersecurity requirements on their supply chain, creating increasing contractual pressure on Vertiv for security documentation, IEC 62443 evidence, SBOM, and coordinated incident reporting [[136]](https://www.lawcode.eu/en/blog/nis2-supply-chain-and-it-security/) [[137]](https://www.nis-2-directive.com/NIS_2_Directive_Article_21.html) [[138]](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/nis2-directive).

### NERC CIP

Vertiv products (UPS protecting BES control centers, KVM switches providing remote access to BES systems, NMCs monitoring critical power infrastructure) are likely deployed in NERC CIP-regulated environments. **CIP-013-2 (Supply Chain Risk Management)** would require Vertiv customers operating Bulk Electric System assets to assess Vertiv's cybersecurity practices, perform vendor risk assessments, and document patch/update processes for Vertiv NMC firmware updates [[116]](https://comnet.net/resources/blog/what-is-nerc-cip-a-guide-to-critical-infrastructure-protection-standards) [[117]](https://www.techtarget.com/searchsecurity/definition/North-American-Electric-Reliability-Corporation-Critical-Infrastructure-Protection-NERC-CIP) [[118]](https://www.certrec.com/resources/info-guides/nerc-standards-nerc-cip-explained-for-the-energy-sector/). **Vertiv has not publicly disclosed any NERC CIP compliance statement, CIP-013 program alignment documentation, or named BES customer deployments.** This represents a sales opportunity: help customers document Vertiv within their CIP-013 programs.

## Strategic Sales Implications for OT Cybersecurity

**The two May 2025 critical CVEs represent the most immediate entry point.** Any organization with deployed Liebert RDU101 or IS-UNITY-DP firmware at v1.9.0.0 or prior (pre-May 2025) in an OT environment — particularly in energy, utilities, healthcare, or data centers with NERC CIP or NIS2 obligations — has a measurable, documented risk. Claroty Team82 discovered these vulnerabilities, suggesting that specialized OT security vendors with UPS/PDU/NMC coverage (Claroty, Nozomi, Dragos) are already tracking Vertiv's embedded network attack surface.

**The CRA compliance gap is the mid-term opportunity.** With full applicability December 11, 2027, Vertiv must complete conformity assessments, publish SBOM, designate EU authorized representatives, and establish ENISA reporting capabilities. The \$10+ billion revenue company has the budget and the regulatory incentive. The lack of IEC 62443-4-1 SDLA certification, absence of SBOM, and single-product IEC 62443-4-2 coverage (only RDU120) indicate a company still building out its product security program — not one that is fully mature.

**The new CIO and refreshed C-suite is the organizational entry point.** Mike Giresi joined June 30, 2025 with explicit responsibility for "AI adoption, cybersecurity, product security, and digital-enabled productivity" [[5]](https://www.vertiv.com/en-asia/about/news-and-events/news-releases/vertiv-names-mike-giresi-as-global-cio/) — a CISO-adjacent mandate for a non-CISO. The new CFO (Craig Chamberlin, November 2025), new CPO (Frieda He, May 2026), and new CTO (Scott Armul, January 2026) all represent relationship development opportunities before incumbent vendor loyalties solidify.

**The \$15.0 billion backlog and \$10.2 billion revenue base confirms Vertiv's centrality to AI data center build-outs**, meaning that any OT cybersecurity product or service that helps Vertiv's hyperscaler and colo customers validate the security of Vertiv power and cooling infrastructure will see an expanding total addressable footprint for the next several years.
