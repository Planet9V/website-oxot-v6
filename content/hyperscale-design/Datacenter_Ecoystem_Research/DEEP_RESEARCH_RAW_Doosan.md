### 5.3 U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission — 10 CFR 73.54

**Confirmed Applicability:** **DOOSAN ENERBILITY PRODUCTS DEPLOYED IN U.S. NUCLEAR FACILITIES ARE SUBJECT TO NRC 10 CFR 73.54 CYBERSECURITY REQUIREMENTS.** [[34]](https://www.doosanenerbility.com/en/business/nuclear_large) [[33]](https://gjia.georgetown.edu/2021/01/22/cyber-security-of-nuclear-power-plants-us-and-global-perspectives/)

**Regulatory Framework:**
- **NRC 10 CFR 73.54** codified in 2009 and updated 2012, mandates cybersecurity plans for U.S. nuclear power plants
- Requires licensees to protect "digital assets" and "safety-critical instrumentation and controls"
- Applies to Doosan-supplied components including:
  - **MMIS (Man-Machine Interface System):** Operator control and monitoring systems
  - **Reactor Pressure Vessels and Steam Generators:** Components with digital monitoring/control
  - **Instrumentation and Control (I&C) Systems:** Real-time sensor acquisition and process control
- **Digital Asset Identification:** Plants must identify which Doosan components/systems fall within digital asset scope
- **Cybersecurity Controls:** Plants must implement specific controls including:
  - Portable media restrictions and scanning requirements
  - Network access controls and monitoring for safety systems
  - Secure configuration baselines
  - Software integrity verification
  - Incident response and reporting procedures
- **Supply Chain Security:** NRC explicitly requires vendor management and security assessments of supplier components

**Doosan's U.S. Nuclear Exposure:**
- **Westinghouse AP1000 partnerships:** Supplied reactor vessels and steam generators under ongoing contracts with U.S. utilities and Westinghouse
- **NuScale Power partnership:** Component manufacturing for NuScale Power Module (NPM) deployments planned in Idaho (Carbon Free Power Project) and other U.S. sites
- **Barakah Nuclear Power Plant (UAE):** Operated by Korean consortium with U.S. engineering oversight; demonstrates manufacturing capability for international standards

**Compliance Implications:** Doosan Enerbility products must maintain design and operational conformance with NRC approved security plans. Any cybersecurity incidents, vulnerabilities, or design changes must be reported through customer (utility) channels. This typically does NOT result in public CVE disclosure but rather confidential customer notifications.
