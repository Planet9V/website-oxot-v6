## Executive Summary

**Schneider Electric SE is the dominant global supplier of energy management and industrial automation infrastructure, with a \$175.7 billion market capitalization and €38.153 billion in FY2024 revenues.** The company holds the #1 position in global data center UPS (24.3% share) and data center power management (21.4% share), and its data center end-market exposure has grown to 30% of total revenues in 2025—up from 24% in 2024 [[12]](https://www.constructionowners.com/news/schneider-electric-sees-data-center-boom-driving-growth) [[13]](https://www.facilitiesdive.com/news/data-centers-remain-standout-industry-for-schneider-electric/813362/) [[68]](https://www.gminsights.com/industry-analysis/data-center-ups-market) [[51]](https://www.verifiedmarketresearch.com/blog/top-data-center-power-management-companies/).

**From an OT cybersecurity sales perspective, Schneider Electric presents both significant opportunity and meaningful risk signals.** The company has disclosed 30+ CVEs affecting EcoStruxure products, Modicon PLCs, and legacy systems in the last 36 months, including a CVSS 9.8 flaw in Modicon M241/M251 controllers and two major ransomware incidents (Cactus ransomware in January 2024, HellCat in November 2024). Concurrently, the company holds some of the strongest OT security certifications in the industry: IEC 62443-4-1 (SDL, TÜV Rheinland, 2019), IEC 62443-4-2 SL2 for its NMC3 (October 2024, industry first), and a brand-new IEC 62443-2-4 for services operations (May 2026) [[78]](https://industrialcyber.co/news/schneider-electric-ecostruxure-nmc3-achieves-iec-62443-4-2-security-level-2-certification/) [[79]](https://blog.se.com/digital-transformation/2026/05/20/iec-62443-2-4-certification-securing-industrial-ot-services-at-scale/) [[81]](https://www.se.com/ww/en/about-us/newsroom/news/press-releases/t%C3%BCv-rheinland-certifies-schneider-electric%E2%80%99s-secure-development-lifecycle-process-to-isa-iec-62443-4-1-5da9d78d8c5665197877d7c7/).

## 1. Company Overview — Foundation, Structure, and Global Footprint

**Full Legal Name:** Schneider Electric SE (Société Européenne — European Company structure) [[102]](https://portersfiveforce.com/blogs/owners/se) [[108]](https://www.se.com/ww/en/about-us/company-profile/history/schneider-electric-history/)

**Founded:** December 27, 1836, when brothers Adolphe and Joseph-Eugène Schneider acquired the Le Creusot iron foundry in Burgundy, France [[104]](https://matrixbcg.com/blogs/brief-history/se). In 1838, the company built France's first steam locomotive. The formal corporate entity Schneider & Compagnie was established on the founding date, making it one of the oldest continuously operating industrial companies in the world [[108]](https://www.se.com/ww/en/about-us/company-profile/history/schneider-electric-history/).

**Corporate Evolution:**
- 1836–1960s: Family-controlled entity under Schneider family governance [[102]](https://portersfiveforce.com/blogs/owners/se)
- 1960s: Absorbed into Belgium's Empain group (renamed Empain-Schneider) [[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schneider_Electric)
- 1981: Paribas acquired controlling stake from Empain family [[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schneider_Electric)
- 1988–1997: Transformative acquisitions — Télémécanique (1988), Square D (1991), Merlin Gerin (1992), Modicon (1997) — establishing the core brand portfolio [[102]](https://portersfiveforce.com/blogs/owners/se) [[105]](https://pestel-analysis.com/blogs/brief-history/se)
- 1999: Officially renamed Schneider Electric, pivoting from steel/engineering to electrical management [[102]](https://portersfiveforce.com/blogs/owners/se) [[104]](https://matrixbcg.com/blogs/brief-history/se)
- 2023: Full acquisition of AVEVA Group plc (GBP 9.86 billion) [[197]](https://schnitgercorp.com/2023/01/18/its-finally-done-schneider-electric-owns-aveva/)

**Headquarters:** Rueil-Malmaison, Île-de-France, France (global HQ) [[102]](https://portersfiveforce.com/blogs/owners/se) [[108]](https://www.se.com/ww/en/about-us/company-profile/history/schneider-electric-history/); US HQ at 800 Federal Street, Andover, Massachusetts 01810 [[106]](https://salestools.io/en/report/schneider-electric-usa-headquarters)

**Global Reach:** Operations in 100+ countries with one million partners [[4]](https://www.se.com/ww/en/about-us/company-profile/) [[108]](https://www.se.com/ww/en/about-us/company-profile/history/schneider-electric-history/)

**Stock Listing:** Euronext Paris — ticker EPA:SU (ISIN: FR0000121972) [[219]](https://live.euronext.com/en/product/equities/FR0000121972-XPAR); American Depositary Receipt (ADR) trades OTC as SBGSY [[103]](https://www.forbes.com/companies/schneider-electric/). **Not SEC-regulated** — listed on Euronext, regulated by the French Autorité des marchés financiers (AMF) [[218]](https://www.se.com/ww/en/about-us/investor-relations/share-information/share-price/) [[220]](https://www.finanzwire.com/press-release/schneider-electric-epa-su-schneider-electric-announces-the-success-of-its-offering-of-bonds-convertible-into-new-shares-andor-exchangeable-for-existing-shares-oceanes-due-2034-for-a-nominal-amount-of-850-million-OgqoiYBbapp).

### EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)

The CRA (Regulation EU 2024/2847) entered into force December 10, 2024 [[165]](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cyber-resilience-act) with the following compliance milestones:

- **September 11, 2026:** Vulnerability and incident reporting obligations begin; 24-hour early warning, 72-hour full notification, 14-day or 1-month final report via ENISA [[164]](https://www.mend.io/blog/eu-cyber-resilience-act-compliance-guide/) [[165]](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cyber-resilience-act)
- **December 11, 2027:** Full compliance mandatory — secure-by-design, SBOM, coordinated vulnerability disclosure, CE marking [[164]](https://www.mend.io/blog/eu-cyber-resilience-act-compliance-guide/)
- **Non-compliance penalties:** Up to €15M or 2.5% of global annual turnover (whichever higher) [[164]](https://www.mend.io/blog/eu-cyber-resilience-act-compliance-guide/)

**Schneider Electric's CRA Scope:** Explicitly confirmed in scope as a manufacturer of "products with digital elements" (PDEs) [[163]](https://blog.se.com/industry/machine-and-process-management/2026/05/14/from-compliance-to-capability-how-europes-cyber-regulations-are-reshaping-machinery-engineering/). ## Strategic Implications for OT Cybersecurity Sales

**Highest-Probability Engagement Opportunities:**

1. **Vulnerability Remediation Programs:** With 30+ CVEs in 36 months — including unpatched flaws in Ecodial and Modicon Communication Server, and EOL products in production (WHC-5918A, PME 2021) — there is a documented, persistent vulnerability backlog across the OT estate that security solution vendors can address directly.

2. **Regulatory Pressure Convergence:** Schneider faces simultaneous compliance obligations under CRA (September 2026 ENISA reporting), NIS2 (supply chain security flow-down to 52,000 suppliers), and NERC CIP (utility deployments). Budget allocation for compliance tooling is a near-term certainty.

3. **Supply Chain Risk Management:** The Bitsight partnership (January 2023) covers supply chain OT exposure, but the 52,000-supplier universe dwarfs the 5,000 receiving active monitoring. Vendors offering scalable third-party OT risk assessment, SBOM verification, or VEX (Vulnerability Exploitability eXchange) tooling are well-positioned.

4. **Federal/Defense Growth Vector:** Schneider Electric Federal, Inc. (established April 2025) with SSA/DCSA approval creates a new buyer persona and procurement pathway with cleared staff requirements and CMMC compliance needs [[148]](https://www.se.com/us/en/about-us/newsroom/news/press-releases/schneider-electric-introduces-u-s-public-sector-business-schneider-electric-federal-inc-67dd90cf59c005c75a001625/).

5. **Data Center Expansion Momentum:** With \$2.3B in US data center deals (November 2025), \$700M in manufacturing investment, and data centers representing 30% of revenue, the operational technology installed at SE customer sites — and within SE's own manufacturing facilities — is expanding rapidly, creating proportional growth in the OT security attack surface requiring protection [[72]](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/schneider-electric-seals-23-billion-us-data-centre-deals-power-ai-boom-2025-11-19/) [[153]](https://www.microgridknowledge.com/about-microgrids/article/55277311/schneider-electric-investing-700m-in-us-operations-through-2027).
