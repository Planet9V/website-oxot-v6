**Security Program Strengths:** Honeywell Building Technologies earned IEC 62443-4-1 certification for its secure software development process in April 2023. The company has a larger number of individual product listings under the ISASecure registry than many other building system competitors.

### 3.2 EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) Exposure

The CRA (effective December 2024, enforcement beginning 2026) places regulatory requirements on Honeywell's digital products:

*   **Experion PKS and Controllers:** Class II critical products with digital elements (PDEs) due to their role in utility grid control.
*   **PRO-Watch Security Systems:** Class II PDEs due to physical access control functions.
*   **IQ4x Building Controllers:** Class II PDEs due to facility operations control.

**Penalty Risks:** Failure to meet CRA requirements for vulnerability disclosures (CRA Article 14) exposing Honeywell to fines up to 2.5% of annual turnover, or approximately $962 million based on FY2024 revenue.

### 3.3 NIS2 supply Chain Obligations

The NIS2 Directive (effective October 2024, full member state enforcement by November 2026) mandates security controls for critical service providers. Honeywell's customers in healthcare, transport, water, and energy must verify their suppliers' security:

*   **SBOM Requirements:** Honeywell must provide CycloneDX/SPDX format software bills of materials for its control software.
*   **Vulnerability Escalate:** Siemens ProductCERT equivalents must disclose vulnerabilities to EU authorities within 24 hours.
*   **Supplier Compliance Program:** In 2025, Honeywell established compliance questionnaires for its own sub-tier suppliers.

### 3.4 Publicly Disclosed Vulnerabilities (Last 36 Months)

*   **CVE-2026-3611 (CVSS 10.0):** Missing authentication vulnerability in IQ4x controllers (IQ4E, IQ412, IQ3, IQECO). Allows unauthenticated remote attackers to execute code and take over the controller. **No patch is available as of March 2026.**
*   **CVE-2026-1670 (CVSS 9.8):** Authentication bypass vulnerability in Honeywell CCTV systems (I-HIB2PI-UL). Allowed remote account takeover. Product was discontinued in April 2025.
*   **CVE-2025-2523 (CVSS 9.4):** Integer underflow in Honeywell OneWireless Wireless Device Manager (WDM) and Experion PKS, leading to remote code execution. Patched in late 2025 updates.

### 3.5 Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) Status

*   **Current Status:** Honeywell requires SBOMs from its sub-contractors and component suppliers, but does not provide a public portal for its own product SBOMs.
*   **Access Protocol:** Customer requests for SBOMs must be coordinated through account managers or submitted via productsecurity@jci.com (wait, productsecurity@jci.com is JCI; Honeywell is PSIRT@honeywell.com).
