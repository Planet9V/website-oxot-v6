# Asetek A/S: Comprehensive Organizational Intelligence Report for B2B Sales

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Asetek A/S is a Danish gaming hardware manufacturer and liquid cooling solutions provider that has undergone significant organizational change. As of April 23, 2026, the company was **delisted from Nasdaq Copenhagen** following a compulsory acquisition by CQXA Holdings Pte. Ltd. (completed April 21, 2026), ending its 26-year history as a publicly traded company [[38]](https://news.cision.com/asetek). 

The company posted **FY2024 revenue of \$71.2 million** across two operating segments—Liquid Cooling (\$42.8M, 60% of revenue) and SimSports (\$9.6M, 13% of revenue)—but reported a comprehensive loss of \$23.9 million [[39]](https://s28.q4cdn.com/535984607/files/doc_news/att-3-Asetek--Q4-2024-Strong-ending-to-a-turnaround-year-2025.pdf). This financial deterioration reflects industry headwinds, declining demand for its core liquid cooling business following patent expiration in May 2025, and the company's strategic pivot toward high-performance gaming peripherals.

**Critical Intelligence for B2B Sales Partners:**

- **Status Change**: Now a private company under Chinese/Singapore ownership (CQXA Holdings); no longer subject to public market disclosure requirements
- **Patent Cliff Impact**: Foundational US patent US8240362B2 (sealed-loop AIO design) expired May 6, 2025, eliminating Asetek's 20-year competitive moat and prompting a formal "strategic review" potentially including asset divestiture [[33]](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/liquid-cooling/aseteks-key-watercooling-patent-expires-today-as-the-company-seeks-to-sell-its-cooling-business) [[35]](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8240362B2/en)
- **Revenue Decline**: Three-year CAGR of **-20.1%** (FY2024 vs FY2023) and **-17.2%** (FY2023 vs FY2022), driven by compressed margins in liquid cooling and competitive losses to Chinese OEMs [[39]](https://s28.q4cdn.com/535984607/files/doc_news/att-3-Asetek--Q4-2024-Strong-ending-to-a-turnaround-year-2025.pdf)
- **Financial Pressure**: Adjusted EBITDA collapsed from \$15.9M (FY2023) to \$0.3M (FY2024); long-term debt stands at \$19.2M against only \$3.3M cash [[39]](https://s28.q4cdn.com/535984607/files/doc_news/att-3-Asetek--Q4-2024-Strong-ending-to-a-turnaround-year-2025.pdf)
- **Business Model**: Asetek does NOT sell consumer cooling products directly; instead, it operates as a Tier-1 technology supplier providing sealed-loop cooling platforms to OEM partners (Corsair, NZXT, EVGA, etc.) who brand and distribute under their own labels
- **Regulatory Scope**: As a consumer/gaming hardware vendor, Asetek is **NOT subject** to industrial cybersecurity frameworks (NERC CIP, NRC 10 CFR 73.54) or IEC 62443 industrial control certifications; these regulatory categories do not apply to the business
