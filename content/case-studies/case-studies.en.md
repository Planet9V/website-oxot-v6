## On disclosure

Every case above is a composite, assembled from the pattern that recurs across multiple engagements of the same kind — not a single identifiable transaction, facility or programme. The sector and the method are real; the specific counterparties, sites and figures are not. A client's name is theirs to disclose, not ours to spend for the sake of a stronger case study, and none has authorised us to use one here. What we publish is the half that belongs to us: what we found, what we changed, and what the work produced.
