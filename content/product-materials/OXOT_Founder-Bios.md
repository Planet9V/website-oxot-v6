## Founder bios

**Vincent de Mildt — Co-Founder (NL).** For over two decades, Vincent has worked
alongside operators and executives across critical infrastructure — listening to their
challenges, understanding their constraints, and watching traditional security approaches
fall short. That proximity to the problem, and a deep commitment to getting it right, led
him to co-found OXOT. Focus: Business Development · Strategic Partnerships · Customer Success.

**Gerben van der Lei — Co-Founder (NL).** A veteran of critical-infrastructure
engineering, Gerben saw firsthand how traditional cybersecurity failed to understand the
physics of industrial systems. His mission: build a security platform that thinks like an
engineer, not a checkbox. Focus: Systems Engineering · Industrial Networks · Threat Modeling.
