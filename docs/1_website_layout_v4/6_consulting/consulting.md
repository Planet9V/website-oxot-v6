## How we work

### Engineers first. The standard is the vocabulary—not the point.

Plant engineers and operators are measured on reliability, safety, throughput, quality, and recovery. They are not measured on control coverage.

That is why OXOT starts with operational reality:

```text
What does this system do?
        ↓
Which functions cannot stop?
        ↓
What protects life, quality, production, service, or mission?
        ↓
What could an attacker actually reach?
        ↓
What can we safely change?
```

IEC 62443 is how OXOT structures and records the answer. It is not the reason for the work.

A report that scores a facility against a framework but cannot tell the plant manager, controls engineer, or operations leader what to do on Monday has not completed the job.

> **The goal is not a better score. The goal is a better decision.**

## The OXOT method

### Collaborative, controlled, and built for action.

Use this as a three-column principle section.

| Principle | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Client-centric** | Plant engineers and operators are measured on reliability and safety, not control coverage. OXOT works collaboratively with the people who understand the system, its constraints, and its recovery requirements. |
| **Controlled execution** | Safety comes first. A named delivery lead sits between the customer, OXOT consultants, specialist teams, and any test activity. Scope, methods, evidence handling, stop conditions, and escalation routes are agreed before work begins. |
| **Actionable outcomes** | The outcome is a prioritized, evidence-backed action plan: quick wins, scheduled work, strategic architecture decisions, and documented risk acceptance where appropriate. Engineering should be able to begin on Monday. |

### The engagement sequence

```text
1. Define the decision
   What risk, change, investment, supplier, system, or assurance question
   needs an answer?

2. Establish the evidence
   Gather approved engineering, operational, asset, topology, configuration,
   safety, reliability, supplier, and lifecycle records.

3. Build the working model
   Connect the facility, assets, control systems, network pathways,
   dependencies, operational consequence, and existing controls.

4. Test options
   Compare remediation, segmentation, access, patch, replacement, supplier,
   modernization, or acceptance options in the Twin.

5. Deliver a decision that holds
   Provide the rationale, evidence, actions, accountable owners,
   sequencing, residual risk, and review triggers.
```

**What you receive**

```text
System and asset view

Prioritized action queue
Quick wins and strategic recommendations
Risk-treatment rationale
Evidence and decision record
```

### 03 — Architecture & Segmentation

> **“Design it so it survives production.”**

Zones and conduits that operators can run, maintainers can work in, and security teams can explain.

- Test segmentation, virtual firewalls, DMZ changes, access restrictions, and migration sequences in the Twin.
- Preserve required operational, diagnostic, safety, maintenance, and recovery flows.
- Create a design that can be implemented and validated in planned operational windows.

**What you receive**

### 04 — Secure Remote Access

> **“Stop the risk in vendor access.”**

The OEM, integrator, maintainer, or specialist still gets the access they need—through a route you can approve, observe, revoke, and defend.

**What we do**

- Map every remote-support route: vendor portals, VPNs, jump hosts, cellular gateways, maintenance laptops, cloud tools, OEM tunnels, and engineering paths.
- Identify which routes can reach control systems, safety-related functions, critical equipment, configuration tools, or sensitive operational data.
- Separate required support access from persistent or unnecessary exposure.
- Design brokered, named-user, MFA-protected, approved, time-limited, recorded, asset-specific access.
- Test controls against maintenance, diagnostic, update, emergency-recovery, and vendor-support workflows.
- Define accountabilities, approvals, logging, access expiry, emergency paths, and evidence requirements.

**What you receive**

```text
Remote-access inventory and pathway map
Vendor and integrator access-risk assessment
Future-state access architecture
Required operational-support flow model
Session approval and accountability design
Implementation roadmap and evidence pack
```

**Best for**

- Plants with legacy vendor VPNs or persistent support connections.
- Rail signaling, CBTC, PTC, depot, or traction-power support environments.
- Water and wastewater remote telemetry or integrator access.
- Data-center BMS/EPMS, generator, cooling, or OEM support.
- Defense/government facilities requiring sovereign or air-gapped access patterns.

**CTA:** `Discuss secure remote access`

# IEC 62443 underneath the services

## The architecture underneath all six services.

Every consulting service rests on the same system model:

```text
System under consideration
        ↓
Assets and operational functions
        ↓
Zones and conduits
        ↓
Cyber pathways and dependencies
        ↓
Consequence and target protection
        ↓
Control design, implementation, and evidence
```

IEC 62443 provides the vocabulary and engineering structure for this work:

- Define the system under consideration.
- Understand assets, functions, interfaces, and operational boundaries.
- Partition the environment into zones and conduits.
- Assess risk according to realistic consequence and pathway.
- Establish target security levels where applicable.
- Define controls and security requirements.
- Maintain evidence through operation and change.

> **Get the boundary wrong, and every control after it protects the wrong thing.**

# Vendor neutral. Evidence owned by you.

## Engagements designed to end.

The best consulting engagement leaves the customer stronger, not more dependent.

OXOT works with customer engineers rather than at them. The people responsible for safety, reliability, controls, operations, IT, procurement, security, and budget should be able to see the reasoning behind a decision.

Nobody is being audited for the sake of it. Findings are argued from evidence, not authority.

```text
Vendor-neutral
No product resale agenda.

Passive-first
No agents on controllers and no active scanning of production networks.

Evidence you own
Your system model, source material, decision rationale, and outputs
remain available to your team.

Designed for handover
The method, model, and reasoning stay when the engagement ends.
```
