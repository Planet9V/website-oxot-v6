## The four decisions in defense language

| OXOT question | Defense & government wording |
|---|---|
| What do we fix first? | Which reachable pathway can degrade mission support, essential government services, base operations, secure facilities, or logistics? |
| What should we spend? | Should we invest in sovereign hosting, power resilience, alternate communications, secure vendor access, spares, fuel capacity, or supplier diversification? |
| Can we change safely? | Can we alter a firewall, remote-support route, generator-control system, identity boundary, firmware package, or communications path without reducing readiness? |
| What can we leave alone? | Which legacy asset or dependency can remain under compensating controls until replacement, with a documented mission-risk rationale? |
