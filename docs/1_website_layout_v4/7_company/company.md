### Mission

> ## Protect the infrastructure that sustains society.
>
> Our mission is to protect the critical infrastructure that sustains society and powers progress—for the generations after us.
>
> Critical systems cannot simply be scanned, patched, restarted, or segmented because a generic security tool says so. Every change must account for process constraints, safety, reliability, maintenance, suppliers, recovery, and the physical consequence of getting it wrong.

### Why OXOT exists

> ## Cybersecurity should begin with consequence—not a score.
>
> A vulnerability list cannot tell you what matters most in a live operational environment. A critical vulnerability on an unreachable asset is not the same as a reachable pathway to a safety-related controller, signaling engineering environment, water-treatment system, cooling plant, or critical power function.
>
> OXOT begins with the system itself: what it does, what it depends on, what can reach it, and what happens if it fails or is manipulated.

```text
        ↓
Cyber reachability
        ↓

        ↓
Evidence-backed decision
```

### Five things that hold

> ## Five principles, whichever system you operate.

| Principle | Copy |
|---|---|
| **Founded by practitioners** | OXOT was founded by former Fox-IT and NCC Group OT security leads. We understand that a good recommendation must survive engineering review, live operations, vendor constraints, maintenance windows, and executive scrutiny. |
| **Operational Technology** | OT is not office IT copied onto the plant floor. It includes control systems, physical processes, safety barriers, long-lived assets, human operators, suppliers, and consequences beyond data loss. |
| **Risk-based method** | OXOT uses a consequence-led, risk-based method engineered to IEC 62443 principles. We model systems, assets, zones, conduits, pathways, controls, and operational constraints before recommending action. |
| **Sovereignty** | Your data and your model stay yours. OXOT supports isolated, data-diode, and dedicated single-tenant deployment approaches for sensitive and sovereign environments. |
| **Vendor-neutral** | We do not begin with a preferred product or reseller agreement. We compare controls and options against the same modeled system, pathway, consequence, and operational constraint. |

### How OXOT works

> ## One system model. Better decisions.
>
> OXOT combines OT cybersecurity consulting with the Cyber Digital Twin. We build the model with your teams from the engineering and operational evidence you already hold—then use it to test decisions before they affect the live environment.

```text
1. Understand the environment
   Engineering evidence, assets, topology, configuration, dependencies.

2. Build the Twin
   Process/facility, controls, networks, suppliers, pathways, and consequences.

3. Test the decision
   Segmentation, vendor access, patching, replacement, supplier, or investment.

4. Produce evidence
   A traceable rationale for engineering, operations, security, safety,
   procurement, assurance, and leadership.
```

### Sovereignty and vendor neutrality

> ## Your model stays under your control.
>
> Critical-infrastructure evidence can include OT topology, control configurations, safety information, supplier dependencies, recovery procedures, and sensitive operational constraints. OXOT supports passive-first deployment models that do not require agents on PLCs, RTUs, or controllers, and do not actively scan production networks. 

> The Cyber Digital Twin can run in a customer-controlled isolated environment, receive approved intelligence through a one-way data diode, or operate in a dedicated single-tenant instance aligned to data-sovereignty requirements.
