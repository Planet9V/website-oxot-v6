# Privacy Policy

OXOT respects your privacy and processes personal data in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Dutch law. This policy explains what we process when you use this website, why, and what rights you have.

This is a general template. It has not been reviewed by legal counsel — have it reviewed before relying on it.

Last updated 2026-08-07

Who is responsible
The data controller is Oxot B.V. For any question about this policy or how we handle your personal data, write to info@oxot.nl.

What we collect
We keep collection to what we genuinely need. This site has no newsletter, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

The 2-minute check. The answers you give are processed in your browser to produce the verdict on screen. Nothing is sent to us for the on-screen answer.
Requesting the report. If you ask for the PDF, we process your name and work email address, together with the answers you gave, so we can prepare and follow up on the report.
The written review and contact. When you write to us we process the name, email address, organisation, role and message you provide, so we can answer.
Abuse prevention. Your IP address is used momentarily to rate-limit submissions. It is stored only as a salted, irreversible hash — never in readable form.
Server logs. Like most websites, our infrastructure records technical data needed for security and reliability.
Why, and on what legal basis
To answer your enquiry and prepare what you asked for — legal basis: steps taken at your request prior to a contract, or our legitimate interest.
To keep the site secure and available, including rate limiting — legal basis: our legitimate interest.
To remember your language and theme — legal basis: strictly necessary for a service you requested.
How long we keep it
Only as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected for, or as required by law. Enquiries are kept for the duration of our contact and a reasonable follow-up period.

Who we share it with
We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with providers who process it on our behalf — hosting and infrastructure — under a processing agreement, and where we have configured a notification service, to alert us that you wrote.

Your rights
You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing already carried out. Write to info@oxot.nl and we will respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Transfers outside the EEA
We prefer providers in the European Economic Area. Where data is processed outside it, we rely on an adequacy decision or on Standard Contractual Clauses
